# Season 4, Chapter 12: THE BOTTOM LINE
## Ensemble | FL Integration: Strong

Each friend reflects on what they gained beyond money — skills, confidence, relationships, purpose. They choose to keep the business running sustainably rather than scaling aggressively or shutting down. The map glows, showing golden threads connecting every customer, the café, the fort, Mr. Mason's workshop, Frances's garden — a living network of relationships. Dim spots remain in unexplored parts of the neighborhood, hinting at future growth.
