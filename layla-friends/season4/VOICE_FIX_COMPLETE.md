## Key Transformations Applied

### Kid Dialogue Fixes

**Example 1:**
**Before:** "It's too emotionally draining constantly trying to satisfy other people's ever-changing expectations."
**After:** "I'm so tired of redoing stuff when she keeps changing her mind."

**Example 2:**
**Before:** "I want the team that takes the time to do the job correctly the first time, even if it costs more."
**After:** "I want people who do it right the first time, even if it costs more."

### Narrator Simplification

**Before:** "meticulously tracking every request"
**After:** "tracking every request carefully"

**Before:** "characteristically quick to propose solutions when faced with challenges"
**After:** "always quick with ideas when they faced problems"

**Before:** "woefully inadequate compensation"
**After:** "way too little money"

**Before:** "reconcile his finances"
**After:** "figure out his money"

**Before:** "the business model's viability"
**After:** "whether this could actually work"

**Before:** "letting the harsh economic calculation hang in the still air without additional commentary"
**After:** "letting the math sink in"

**Before:** "universally exhausted expressions"
**After:** "exhausted faces"

**Before:** "simultaneously helpful and productively busy"
**After:** "busy and happy to help"

**Before:** "pondered the deeper meaning"
**After:** "thought about what Frances meant"

**Before:** "mortifying mistake"
**After:** "embarrassing mistake"

**Before:** "tested their collective resolve in ways none of them had anticipated"
**After:** "tested them in ways they hadn't expected"

**Before:** "The veteran craftsman listened patiently without interrupting, his expression thoughtful rather than alarmed"
**After:** "Mr. Mason listened patiently to every detail"

**Before:** "Their established and growing reputation for quality craftsmanship had attracted this valuable new customer entirely and exclusively through word-of-mouth referral"
**After:** "Their reputation for quality work had brought this customer through word of mouth"

**Before:** "experiencing a profound mixture of relief, pride, and hard-earned wisdom about how markets actually function"
**After:** "feeling relieved, proud, and wiser about how quality matters in business"
