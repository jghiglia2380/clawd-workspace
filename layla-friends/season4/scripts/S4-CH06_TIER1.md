## HIDDEN COSTS
### Scene 1 (33 words)
Ellis was cutting wood when his saw blade broke. Snap! Two pieces fell on the table. "I was being careful!" he said. Mr. Mason looked at the blade. "Tools wear out. That's not your fault."
### Scene 2 (32 words)
They went to the hardware store. A new blade cost twelve dollars. Ellis paid for it, but he was upset. "We didn't plan for this," he said. It wasn't in the budget.
### Scene 3 (33 words)
Riley opened her kiln. Three perfect mugs sat inside. She smiled. Then she picked up her glaze jar. Almost empty. Her smile went away. "I need more glaze," she said quietly. Another surprise cost.
### Scene 4 (32 words)
At the store, the small glaze jar cost eighteen dollars. A big jar cost forty-five dollars. "The big one lasts three times longer," the shop owner said. Riley always bought small.
### Scene 5 (33 words)
At the fort meeting, the mood was serious. Ellis had his twelve dollar receipt. Riley had her eighteen dollar receipt. "That's thirty dollars we didn't plan for," Layla said. Nobody knew where it should come from.
### Scene 6 (32 words)
Layla looked at their pricing sheet. Every material was listed for each product. But saw blades were not on any list. Glaze refills were not either. "We missed these costs," Layla said slowly.
### Scene 7 (33 words)
They asked Mr. Mason about these surprise costs. He showed them a paper with neat numbers. "This is my overhead sheet," he said. "Every business has costs you don't see at first." The friends looked closely.
### Scene 8 (32 words)
Layla read the sheet. Rent. Electricity. New tools. Sandpaper. "You pay for all of this?" she asked. Mr. Mason nodded. "The lights cost money. The heat costs money. It all adds up."
### Scene 9 (33 words)
"We use your workshop," Layla said. "Your electricity. Your lights." Mr. Mason smiled. "I don't mind sharing. But electricity costs thirty to forty dollars a month." Overhead is what it costs just to keep a business going.
### Scene 10 (32 words)
Mr. Mason held up a big box of sandpaper. "Fifty sheets for fifteen dollars. That's thirty cents each." Then a small box. "Ten sheets for eight dollars. Eighty cents each." Buying big saved money.
### Scene 11 (33 words)
Walking home, Benny said, "But we didn't pay for electricity. We used Mr. Mason's." Layla shook her head. "We didn't pay, but someone did. We should count these costs. They are real."
### Scene 12 (32 words)
At the fort, Layla said something hard. "We need to figure out our true profit." The profit jar showed two hundred seventeen dollars. That number felt good. Nobody wanted it to change.
### Scene 13 (33 words)
Layla listed the hidden costs. Saw blade wear: six dollars. Glaze savings lost by buying small: twelve dollars. Electricity for four weeks: twenty-two dollars. "That's sixty-two dollars we never counted," she said quietly.
### Scene 14 (32 words)
Layla reached into the PROFIT jar. She moved sixty-two dollars to the COSTS jar. Everyone watched. The profit jar went from two hundred seventeen dollars to one hundred fifty-five dollars. The room was quiet.
### Scene 15 (33 words)
"Did we lose money?" Benny asked. Layla shook her head. "No. We still made money. One hundred fifty-five dollars is real profit. Before, we thought we made more. But that wasn't the true number."
### Scene 16 (32 words)
They changed their prices. Layla added fifteen percent for overhead. "That covers tools wearing out and electricity," she said. They wrote the new prices on the pricing sheet together. It felt right.
### Scene 17 (33 words)
Riley decided to buy the big glaze jar. It cost more now but lasted three times longer. "That saves thirty percent," Layla said. Spending more now saved money later. Riley liked that idea.
### Scene 18 (32 words)
They put ten dollars in a new jar called RESERVE. "This is for replacing tools," Layla said. Ellis looked glad. Next time a blade broke, the money would already be there.
### Scene 19 (33 words)
Layla made a special list in her notebook. She called it: "Costs That Are Hard to See." Tools. Electricity. Buying in bulk. "We will check this every month," she said. Ellis nodded. Planning felt better.
### Scene 20 (32 words)
That night, Layla looked at the map. The bright golden lines still glowed. But now she saw gray shadows under them. Hidden costs. They were always there. Now she could see the whole picture.
