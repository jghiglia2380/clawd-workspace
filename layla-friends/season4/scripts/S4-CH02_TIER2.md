## THE BUSINESS PLAN

### Scene 1 (45 words)
The friends sat in a circle at the fort, trying to pick a name for their team. Benny wanted "Benny's Bunch." Riley suggested "Art Kids." Ellis said "Maplewood Makers" because they lived on Maplewood Lane and made things. They voted, and Maplewood Makers won.

### Scene 2 (45 words)
"Let's go find customers right now!" Benny said, jumping up from his seat. But Layla held up her hand. "Wait. We need a plan first," she said. "Last time we just ran around with no plan. It was a mess." Riley nodded. "Layla is right."

### Scene 3 (45 words)
They walked together to Mr. Mason's workshop to ask for help. His workshop smelled like sawdust and wood stain. They told him about Maplewood Makers. Mr. Mason listened carefully and then asked one question. "What will it cost you to make these things?"

### Scene 4 (45 words)
Mr. Mason opened a large book on his workbench. "This is my ledger," he said. "Every piece of wood I buy goes in here, and every jar of stain too." Ellis leaned closer to see the neat rows of numbers. "You write down everything?" he asked.

### Scene 5 (45 words)
"Here is the most important part," Mr. Mason said. He wrote three words on a piece of paper: PRICE minus COST equals PROFIT. "The price is what people pay you. The cost is what you spent. The profit is what you get to keep."

### Scene 6 (45 words)
Mr. Mason told them about a time his best saw broke during a big job. He had to buy a new one fast, and it ate the profit from two projects. "That is why you always save a little money for surprises," he said.

### Scene 7 (45 words)
Back at the fort, Ellis opened his notebook to a fresh page. "Let's list everything we can make," he said. Riley said pottery and mugs. Ellis said shelves and repairs. Layla said journals. Benny said deliveries. Ellis wrote each one down in a neat column.

### Scene 8 (45 words)
Ellis picked the shelf to try first. He listed the costs out loud while writing them down. "Wood costs four dollars. Screws cost one dollar. Sandpaper costs fifty cents. Stain costs fifty cents." He added it all together. "Six dollars to make one shelf," he said proudly.

### Scene 9 (45 words)
"So how much should we charge?" Ellis asked the group. Benny said eight dollars because it sounded fair. Layla said twelve because they should earn more than a little. Ellis said fifteen because their time had value too. They talked it over and agreed on fifteen dollars.

### Scene 10 (45 words)
On the way home, they stopped at Frances's garden. She was watering her sunflowers when they told her about the business. Frances smiled and asked a question they had not thought about. "Who are your customers? A garden grows best when you know what the soil needs."

### Scene 11 (45 words)
The friends thought about the question on the walk back to the fort. "People who need things fixed," Ellis said. "People who want something handmade and special," Riley added. Layla wrote the ideas down in her notebook. "Anyone in the neighborhood who needs something made or fixed."

### Scene 12 (45 words)
Next they figured out who would do what on the team. It came together quickly because everyone had different strengths. Layla was organized, so she would handle money and planning. Ellis liked building. Riley was the artist. Benny loved talking to people, so he would handle sales.

### Scene 13 (45 words)
Riley grabbed a big piece of paper and drew four columns with a marker. She wrote each name at the top. Under Layla she put "manager and money." Under Ellis she put "building and repairs." Under her own name she wrote "pottery and design." Benny got "sales and delivery."

### Scene 14 (45 words)
Then Ellis added up everything they would need to buy before they could start. Wood, clay, paint, sandpaper, screws, and stain. The list kept growing as they thought of more items. When he finished the math, the total was sixty dollars. "That is our startup cost," Ellis said.

### Scene 15 (45 words)
"We still have money from last week," Layla said. They had fifty-eight dollars from their solo work. Everyone put in a little more from their allowances until they had enough. Sixty dollars sat on the table. It was just enough to start Maplewood Makers for real.

### Scene 16 (45 words)
The next morning, Layla brought three clear jars to the fort. She taped a label on each one. The first jar said COSTS. The second said PROFIT. The third said RESERVE. They put fifty dollars in the costs jar and ten in the reserve jar for surprises.

### Scene 17 (45 words)
Layla also made a simple order form for customers. It had spaces for the customer name, what they wanted made, the price they agreed on, and when it needed to be done. "Now we will not forget anything," she said. Ellis grinned. "No more payment mix-ups."

### Scene 18 (45 words)
Ellis finished the pricing sheet by writing down every product they planned to sell. Each line showed the item, what it cost to make, how long it took, and the selling price. The last column showed profit. "We can earn real money with this plan," Ellis said.

### Scene 19 (45 words)
Mr. Mason came by the fort to check on them that afternoon. They showed him the pricing sheet, the roles chart, and the three jars. He studied everything and nodded. "You have got a solid plan," he said. "Now go do it, and track everything you spend."

### Scene 20 (45 words)
After Mr. Mason left, the friends looked at everything on the walls. The pricing sheet was taped up high. The jars sat on the shelf in a neat row. The roles chart hung near the door. They were ready. Layla smiled. "Maplewood Makers is open for business."

## FL VOCABULARY INTRODUCED

1. **cost** (Scenes 3, 5, 8, 14) — The money spent to make something
2. **price** (Scenes 5, 9, 17) — What a customer pays for an item
3. **profit** (Scenes 5, 16, 18) — The money left over after paying costs
4. **ledger** (Scene 4) — A book for tracking money spent and earned
5. **startup cost** (Scene 14) — The money needed to begin a business
