**Target Narration Speed:** 75 WPM
**Target Word Count:** 600 words
**Episode Duration:** ~8 minutes
**Character Focus:** Benny (spotlight)
**FL Integration:** Medium (capacity, overcommitting, too much work)
**Setting:** Fort, neighborhood

## THE BIG MISTAKE

### Scene 1 (30 words)
Monday was a normal day. The board had four jobs on it. Everyone knew what to do. "Good week ahead," said Layla. They did not know what Benny was about to do.

### Scene 2 (30 words)
Benny met Mrs. Garcia at the park. She needed two shelves by Friday. "Sure!" said Benny. He said yes without asking the team first. He just wanted to help.

### Scene 3 (30 words)
On Tuesday, Benny said yes to three more people. He wrote the orders on little papers. He put them in his pocket. He was happy. He thought more work meant more money.

### Scene 4 (30 words)
At the fort, Benny read the new orders out loud. Seven new ones! Plus four they already had. Eleven total! "We can not do eleven," said Layla. Benny's smile went away.

### Scene 5 (30 words)
"We can do about six in a week," said Layla. "Eleven is way too many." Benny looked at the floor. He had not thought about the numbers before saying yes.

### Scene 6 (30 words)
"But I already told them yes!" said Benny. "I can not say no now." Nobody wanted to break a promise. But nobody knew how to do all the work either.

### Scene 7 (30 words)
They decided to try. They would work extra hours. They would go fast. "It will be hard," said Ellis. He did not know how hard it would really be.

### Scene 8 (30 words)
The rest of the week was a mess. They worked all day long. Sawdust was everywhere. Wet glaze dripped on the table. Nobody took a real break. Everyone was tired.

### Scene 9 (30 words)
Riley had to rush a special platter. She usually took eight hours. This time she had three. It came out okay but not her best. She felt sad about it.

### Scene 10 (30 words)
Ellis cut a board too short because he was going too fast. He had to throw it away and start over. "I was rushing," he said. That wasted wood and time.

### Scene 11 (30 words)
One order was not done on Friday. The customer called. Benny said, "Can we bring it tomorrow?" The customer was not happy. Benny felt bad about the broken promise.

### Scene 12 (30 words)
Saturday, they worked for ten hours straight. By the end, everyone's hands were sore. They finished all the orders just as the sun was going down. It was a very long day.

### Scene 13 (30 words)
One order went out late on Sunday. The customer took it but looked upset. "I expected this yesterday," she said. Benny said he was sorry. He meant it.

### Scene 14 (30 words)
They counted the money. One hundred thirty-two dollars! A record! But nobody cheered. They were too tired. Riley put her head on the table. Even Benny was quiet.

### Scene 15 (30 words)
Layla did the math. They worked fifty-two hours total. The profit per hour was only one dollar and seventy-one cents. That was less than before! More work did not mean more pay.

### Scene 16 (30 words)
Mr. Mason came by and saw how tired they were. He sat next to Benny. "When you promise too much," he said, "the promise breaks AND the work gets worse."

### Scene 17 (30 words)
Layla made a special card for Benny. Green meant "take more orders." Yellow meant "almost full." Red meant "no more this week." Benny would carry the card when he talked to people.

### Scene 18 (30 words)
They tried it out. The card was set to yellow. Benny practiced saying, "We are full this week. Can I put you on the list for next week?" It worked!

### Scene 19 (30 words)
Benny looked at the card. "I just wanted to help everyone," he said quietly. "But I made everyone tired instead." He looked at his friends. "I am sorry."

### Scene 20 (30 words)
The map stopped flickering and glowed steady again. Benny put the card on the table. "Green means go," he said. "Red means we need to grow first." Everyone smiled.

1. **capacity** (Scene 5) — The most work you can do in a certain time
2. **profit per hour** (Scene 15) — How much money you earn for every hour you work
