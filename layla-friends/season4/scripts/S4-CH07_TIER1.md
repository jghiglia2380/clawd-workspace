## THE BIG MISTAKE

### Scene 1 (33 words)
Monday was a good day. The board had four jobs on it. That was the right amount. Ellis checked his tools. Riley drew a picture for a platter. Benny went to see the neighbors.

### Scene 2 (32 words)
Benny saw Mrs. Garcia. "Can you fix my shelves?" she asked. "Yes!" said Benny. He did not check with the team first. He just wanted to help. He said yes too fast.

### Scene 3 (33 words)
The next morning, Benny talked to three more people. They all wanted things made. Benny said yes to everyone. That was three more jobs. He did not tell his friends about them yet.

### Scene 4 (32 words)
Benny came to the fort. "I got us seven new jobs!" he said. He smiled big. But nobody else smiled. Layla looked at the board. "Seven more?" she asked. "On top of four?"

### Scene 5 (33 words)
Layla counted. Four plus seven was eleven. "We can do about six jobs in a week," she said. "Eleven is way too many." She looked at Benny. "We can't do all of this."

### Scene 6 (32 words)
Benny felt bad. "But I already said yes," he told them. "I promised." He looked at the floor. He did not want to break his promises. But he had promised too much.

### Scene 7 (33 words)
"We have to try," said Ellis. "If we break promises, people won't trust us." So they decided to do all eleven jobs. Riley looked at her platter drawing. She needed lots of time for it.

### Scene 8 (32 words)
The next few days were crazy. Everyone worked from morning until dark. Nobody stopped to eat lunch. Nobody told jokes. The fort did not feel fun anymore. It felt like too much work.

### Scene 9 (33 words)
Riley had to rush her platter for Mrs. Davis. She only had three hours. She needed eight. She went fast. She skipped the pretty parts. When she held it up, it looked just okay.

### Scene 10 (32 words)
Ellis cut a board too short. He was tired and not paying attention. The wood was ruined. He had to throw it away and start over. That cost them four dollars.

### Scene 11 (33 words)
Two jobs were due on Friday. One was not done. Benny had to call the customer. "Can we bring it tomorrow?" he asked. The customer did not sound happy. Benny felt terrible.

### Scene 12 (32 words)
Saturday they worked from seven in the morning until five at night. Ellis dropped a birdhouse and broke it. Riley's hands hurt. Layla could not keep her eyes open. Everyone was so tired.

### Scene 13 (33 words)
Sunday morning, Benny brought the last order. The customer frowned. "This was supposed to come yesterday." Benny said sorry. Walking home, he kicked a rock. He wanted to make people happy. He didn't.

### Scene 14 (32 words)
They counted the money. One hundred thirty-two dollars! The most ever. "We did it!" said Benny. But nobody cheered. They were too tired. Riley rubbed her sore hands. Ellis put his head down.

### Scene 15 (33 words)
Layla did the math. They worked fifty-two hours total. After paying for supplies and wasted wood, they made eighty-nine dollars. That was only one dollar and seventy-one cents for each hour of work.

### Scene 16 (32 words)
"Before this week, we made about three dollars an hour," said Layla. "This week we made less." Ellis frowned. "We worked harder but earned less?" Layla nodded. "Rushing made us waste things."

### Scene 17 (33 words)
Mr. Mason came by. Benny told him what happened. "If you promise more than you can do, that's two mistakes," said Mr. Mason. "The promise you can't keep, and the work that suffers."

### Scene 18 (32 words)
Layla had an idea. She made a card for Benny. One side was green. That meant they could take more work. The other side was red. That meant they were full.

### Scene 19 (33 words)
They practiced. Benny pretended someone asked for a shelf. He looked at the card. It was red. "We're full this week," he said. "But I can put you on the list for next week."

### Scene 20 (32 words)
Benny put the card in his pocket. "I wanted everyone to be happy," he said. "But I made everyone tired instead." Riley smiled. "The card will help you help people the right way."
