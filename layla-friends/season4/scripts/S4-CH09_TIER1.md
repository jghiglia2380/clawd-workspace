## THE PARTNERSHIP

### Scene 1 (30 words)
A woman came to the fort on Saturday morning. Her name was Ellen. She owned the café on the corner. "I have an idea for you," she said with a smile.

### Scene 2 (30 words)
"I want to sell your pottery at my café," said Ellen. "People see my mugs and ask about them." Riley got excited. Her mugs in a real store!

### Scene 3 (30 words)
"So you want to buy our mugs?" asked Benny. "Not exactly," said Ellen. "I show them. When one sells, we share the money." The team looked confused. That was new.

### Scene 4 (30 words)
They went to Mr. Mason for help. He said, "That is called consignment. Ellen shows the mugs. When one sells, she keeps part of the money and gives you the rest."

### Scene 5 (30 words)
"If she just bought them, she would pay you now," said Mr. Mason. "But with consignment, she only pays you after a customer buys one. You still own them until then."

### Scene 6 (30 words)
The team talked about it. Ellen was giving them space in her café. She was telling her customers about them. "That is worth something," said Layla. They thought consignment was fair.

### Scene 7 (30 words)
Ellen said she would keep forty percent. Benny was not sure if that was fair. "Can we think about it?" he asked. Ellen said, "Of course. Take your time."

### Scene 8 (30 words)
That night they did the math. A mug cost twelve dollars. At forty percent, Ellen kept four dollars and eighty cents. At thirty percent, she kept three dollars and sixty cents. They wanted something lower.

### Scene 9 (30 words)
Benny went back to Ellen. "What if you keep thirty percent for the first month? Then thirty-five after that?" Ellen smiled. "That works for me." They shook hands on the deal.

### Scene 10 (30 words)
"We should write it down," said Layla. "If it is not on paper, someone might forget." They agreed. A written deal was safer than just a handshake.

### Scene 11 (30 words)
Layla wrote the rules on a page. The products, the percentages, and what happens if mugs do not sell. Layla and Ellen both signed it. It was official!

### Scene 12 (30 words)
Riley picked six of her best mugs. She chose warm colors that would look nice in a café. She cleaned each one and wrapped them up carefully. "These are ready," she said.

### Scene 13 (30 words)
They brought the mugs to the café. Ellen put them on a shelf near the counter. A small sign said MADE BY MAPLEWOOD MAKERS. Benny just stared at it. Their mugs in a store!

### Scene 14 (30 words)
The week went by slowly. Benny walked past the café every day and peeked in the window. Were the mugs still there? Had anyone bought one? He could not tell.

### Scene 15 (30 words)
On Friday, Ellen called. "I sold three mugs this week!" she said. Benny almost dropped the phone. Three mugs! He ran to tell the team. Everyone cheered.

### Scene 16 (30 words)
Saturday, they went to get the money. Three mugs at twelve dollars made thirty-six dollars. Ellen kept her share. The team got twenty-five dollars and twenty cents. Benny counted it carefully.

### Scene 17 (30 words)
They put the café money in a special jar. "We keep it separate," said Layla. "So we know how much comes from the café." The little jar felt very important.

### Scene 18 (30 words)
"We made money at school," said Benny. "The mugs sold when we were not even there!" Layla smiled. "That is what a partnership does. Both sides win."

### Scene 19 (30 words)
Frances came by the fort. "Partnerships work when both people get what they need," she said. "Ellen gets pottery. You get new customers. That is a good deal."

### Scene 20 (30 words)
The map showed a new line. It was thicker and a different color. It went from the fort to the café. Smaller lines came off it, reaching new people. The network was growing.
