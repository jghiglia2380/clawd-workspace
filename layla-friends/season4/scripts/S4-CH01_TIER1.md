# CHAPTER 1: GOING SOLO - TIER 1 (K-1ST GRADE)

**Reading Levels:** D
**Target Narration Speed:** 75 WPM
**Target Word Count:** 600 words
**Episode Duration:** ~8 minutes
**Character Focus:** All four friends
**FL Integration:** Helping friends, working together
**Setting:** Secret fort and neighborhood

---

## GOING SOLO

### Scene 1 (30 words)
The friends met at their fort. Ellis had a notebook. He showed them a list. "Fourteen people want our help," he said. That was a lot of people!

### Scene 2 (30 words)
"Let's split it up," said Riley. She would make pottery. Ellis would fix things. Layla would sell journals. Benny would run errands. They each picked a job to do alone.

### Scene 3 (30 words)
Riley went to see Mrs. Patterson. "I want six mugs that match," Mrs. Patterson said. Riley said yes. But she did not ask what "match" meant. That might be a problem.

### Scene 4 (30 words)
Ellis went to fix a gate. He had his tools. But he needed a different saw. "I don't have it," he told Mr. Chen. He had to come back later.

### Scene 5 (30 words)
Layla sat in the park with her journals. She put them on a blanket. People walked by and looked. They smiled. But no one bought one. Layla felt sad.

### Scene 6 (30 words)
Benny ran errands for neighbors. He went to the store. He went to the post office. He was very busy! But he forgot to write down who paid him.

### Scene 7 (30 words)
The friends met on Thursday. "How is it going?" asked Layla. Everyone said "fine." But they looked tired. Riley had clay on her hands. Working alone was hard.

### Scene 8 (30 words)
Mrs. Patterson did not like the mugs. "Make them taller," she said. Riley made new ones. Then Mrs. Patterson wanted different handles. Riley had to make them again and again.

### Scene 9 (30 words)
Ellis walked a lot between jobs. He fixed something on one street. Then he walked far away to fix something else. He walked more than he worked. That was not good.

### Scene 10 (30 words)
Layla sold one journal! A teacher paid eight dollars. Layla was happy. But the journal took four hours to make. That was only two dollars for each hour.

### Scene 11 (30 words)
Benny mixed up the money. He gave the wrong change to the wrong person. Now he had to use his own money to fix it. That felt really bad.

### Scene 12 (30 words)
Layla visited Frances in her garden. Frances was picking tomatoes. "One plant makes some tomatoes," Frances said. "A whole garden feeds everyone." Layla thought about what that meant.

### Scene 13 (30 words)
Ellis had to say no to new work. He told people he was too busy. He felt bad about it. One person could not do everything alone.

### Scene 14 (30 words)
Riley brought the mugs back again. Mrs. Patterson shook her head. "They are still not right," she said. Riley felt like crying. She did not want to make mugs anymore.

### Scene 15 (30 words)
On Friday night, each friend counted their money. Riley had twenty-two dollars. Ellis had nineteen. Layla had twelve. Benny had five. The money seemed small for so much work.

### Scene 16 (30 words)
Saturday morning, they met at the fort. They put their money together. Fifty-eight dollars. "That seems like a lot," said Benny. But Layla had a question about the hours.

### Scene 17 (30 words)
They added up the hours. Thirty-five hours total! "Fifty-eight dollars for thirty-five hours?" said Ellis. That was less than two dollars for each hour. That was not very much.

### Scene 18 (30 words)
Mr. Mason came by the fort. He saw the money and their tired faces. Ellis told him the numbers. "Less than two dollars an hour," Mr. Mason said quietly.

### Scene 19 (30 words)
Layla looked at her friends. "We all tried to do everything alone," she said. "Maybe that is the problem. What if we worked together as one team instead?"

### Scene 20 (30 words)
"Yes!" said Riley. "Like a real team!" Ellis nodded. Benny grinned. "Let's try it for one week!" They all agreed. They felt happy and ready to try something new.
