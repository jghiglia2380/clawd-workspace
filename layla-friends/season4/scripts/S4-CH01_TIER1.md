## GOING SOLO
### Scene 1 (33 words)
Three weeks had passed since the big Makers' Faire. The friends met at their fort. Ellis showed his notebook. "Fourteen people want us to make things," he said. Everyone felt excited but also a little worried.
### Scene 2 (32 words)
"That is a lot," said Layla. Riley had an idea. "Let's split up the work. I'll make pottery. Ellis can fix things. Layla can make journals. Benny can help neighbors."
### Scene 3 (33 words)
Riley went to see Mrs. Patterson. "I want six mugs," said Mrs. Patterson. "They should all match." Riley said yes. But she did not ask what "match" meant. That was a mistake.
### Scene 4 (32 words)
Ellis went to fix a gate. He brought his tools. But he needed a different saw. "I don't have it," he told Mr. Chen. He had to come back tomorrow.
### Scene 5 (33 words)
Layla went to the park with her journals. She put them on a blanket. People looked at them. They smiled. But nobody bought one. After two hours, Layla went home feeling sad.
### Scene 6 (32 words)
Benny ran errands for neighbors. He went to the store. He went to the post office. He returned library books. Benny felt busy and helpful. But he forgot to write things down.
### Scene 7 (33 words)
The friends met on Thursday. "How is it going?" asked Layla. Everyone said "fine." But they all looked tired. Riley had clay on her hands. Ellis had sawdust in his hair. Working alone was hard.
### Scene 8 (32 words)
Mrs. Patterson did not like the mugs. "Can you make them different?" she asked. Riley made them again. Then Mrs. Patterson wanted something else changed. Riley felt frustrated. She made them a third time.
### Scene 9 (33 words)
Ellis walked a lot between jobs. He fixed one thing. Then he walked across town. Then he walked back. "I walk more than I work," he said. That did not feel good.
### Scene 10 (32 words)
Layla sold one journal! A teacher bought it for eight dollars. Layla was happy. Then she thought about it. The journal took four hours to make. Eight dollars was not very much.
### Scene 11 (33 words)
Benny made a big mistake. He mixed up the money. He gave Mrs. Chen's money to Mr. Patel by accident. Now Benny had to use his own money to fix it. That felt bad.
### Scene 12 (32 words)
Layla visited Frances's garden. She needed to think. Frances was picking tomatoes. "One plant makes some tomatoes," Frances said. "A whole garden feeds everyone." Layla thought about that. What did it mean?
### Scene 13 (33 words)
Ellis had to say no to new work. "I'm too busy," he told people. He felt bad about it. There were only so many hours in the day. He could not do everything alone.
### Scene 14 (32 words)
Riley brought the mugs to Mrs. Patterson again. "These still aren't right," said Mrs. Patterson. Riley wanted to cry. "I don't want to make things for people anymore," she told Ellis later.
### Scene 15 (33 words)
On Friday night, everyone counted money. Riley had twenty-two dollars. Ellis had nineteen dollars. Layla had twelve dollars. Benny had five dollars. The money seemed small. They had all worked so hard.
### Scene 16 (32 words)
Saturday morning, they met at the fort. They put their money together. Fifty-eight dollars total. "That seems like a lot," said Benny. But Layla asked an important question. "How many hours did we work?"
### Scene 17 (33 words)
They added up the hours. Riley worked twelve hours. Ellis worked ten hours. Layla worked seven hours. Benny worked six hours. That was thirty-five hours total! "Fifty-eight dollars for all that work?" said Ellis.
### Scene 18 (32 words)
Mr. Mason came to the fort. He saw the money. "How did it go?" he asked. Ellis told him about the fifty-eight dollars. "That's less than two dollars per hour," Mr. Mason said quietly.
### Scene 19 (33 words)
Layla looked at her friends. Everyone looked tired. The pile of money looked small. "We all tried to do everything alone," she said. "Maybe that was the problem. Maybe we should work together instead."
### Scene 20 (32 words)
"What if we had one business together?" asked Layla. "We could help each other!" Riley smiled. "I like that idea!" Ellis and Benny agreed. "Let's try it!" they all said together.
