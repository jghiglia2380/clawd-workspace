# Season 4, Chapter 10: THE SEASONAL RUSH
## Tier 1 Script (Target: 580-720 words, MSL 4-6)
**SCENE 1**
December came. Mrs. Torres came to the workshop. "Can you make ornaments? Little pottery pieces for my tree?" Riley looked at Layla. "Yes! We can make those."
**SCENE 2**
More people wanted ornaments after that. Six ornaments here. Four ornaments there. Eight more from another family. So many orders came in! Benny's card turned red. "We have too many orders," he said.
**SCENE 3**
Layla called a meeting. "Let's ask people to pay half now. Then we can buy clay and glaze." Riley nodded. Ellis agreed. Benny said okay too. "Half now. Half when they pick it up."
**SCENE 4**
Mrs. Torres came back to order. Six ornaments. Six dollars each. Thirty-six dollars total. "You pay eighteen now. Then eighteen when you pick them up," Layla said. Mrs. Torres gave them the money. It went in the jar.
**SCENE 5**
Riley looked at her list. Fourteen ornaments to make. She made one piece per hour. That meant fourteen hours of work! Her shoulders felt tired just thinking about it. She needed a faster way to work.
**SCENE 6**
Riley tried something new. She made seven ornaments that looked the same. She shaped them all. Then she put glaze on all of them. Then she fired them together. Four hours instead of seven. Making them together was faster!
**SCENE 7**
She made the next seven ornaments the same way. Four more hours. Riley did the math. Eight hours for fourteen ornaments! She saved six whole hours. Making them in groups was so much better. Riley smiled.
**SCENE 8**
"I designed it once," Riley told Layla. "That's the fun part. The part I love. Then I made them all fast. I can be creative AND fast. I don't have to pick!" Layla smiled big.
**SCENE 9**
Ellen called from the café. "All six mugs sold. Can I order eight more?" Layla asked Riley. Riley checked her list. "Yes, but it takes ten days." Ellen said that was fine. The café kept buying more and more.
**SCENE 10**
Week two was super busy. They made one hundred and two dollars that week. The most ever! The board was full of sticky notes. The waitlist got long. Nine orders waiting. So much demand!
**SCENE 11**
Benny learned something new. "We're booked through December twentieth," he told people. "But you can order today and get it on the twenty-second." People said yes. Waiting made them want it even more.
**SCENE 12**
Ellen called again. "Can I get twelve more mugs? My biggest order yet." Riley was making things in batches now. She could do it. "Three batches of four. I'll have them by the nineteenth." Ellen was happy.
**SCENE 13**
Week three was hard work. Riley made pottery in batches. Ellis built racks and fixed tools. Benny delivered orders all over town. Layla wrote down every dollar. One hundred and eighteen dollars that week! A new record!
**SCENE 14**
Week four was different. December twenty-third to the end of the month. After the holiday, people stopped ordering so much. They made eighty-nine dollars. Still good. But less than before. The busy time was ending.
**SCENE 15**
End of month meeting time. Layla counted all the money. "Three hundred ninety-six dollars came in. One hundred fifty-three we spent. That leaves two hundred forty-three dollars profit!" Everyone cheered so loud. They had never seen that much money!
**SCENE 16**
"We should save thirty percent for January," Layla said. The cheering stopped. "Why? We earned it," Riley said. Layla looked serious. "January will be slow. People just spent all their money. We need to save some now."
**SCENE 17**
They took a vote. Layla voted save. Riley voted save. Ellis voted save. Benny voted spend. Three to one. Layla moved seventy-three dollars to the save jar. One hundred seventy dollars left to split. Forty-two dollars and fifty cents each.
**SCENE 18**
Four kids got the same amount. But they all did different things with it. Layla put her money in savings. Ellis bought new tools for woodworking. Riley bought paints and art supplies. Benny wanted to buy treats and games. Same money. All different choices.
**SCENE 19**
Frances stopped by the workshop. She looked at the save jar. Seventy-three dollars inside. "You saved seeds," she said with a smile. "That was smart thinking. Winter is coming. Slow times come after busy times. You'll be ready now."
**SCENE 20**
Layla looked at the map that night. The whole network glowed bright gold. Lines everywhere! Thick lines to the café. Many lines to customers. So much activity! But some lines looked a little bit orange instead of gold. Too hot. The map was showing something important. This pace couldn't keep going. Saving money was the right thing to do.
