## THE SEASONAL RUSH

### Scene 1 (30 words)
December came and the orders started pouring in. "Can you make ornaments?" asked a customer. By the end of the week, four people wanted handmade gifts. The board filled up fast.

### Scene 2 (30 words)
They had orders for twenty-one ornaments! Six from one person, four from another, three from a third, and eight more. Benny looked at his card. It was yellow already. "That is a lot."

### Scene 3 (30 words)
"We need money for supplies," said Layla. "What if people pay half now?" The team agreed. That way they could buy clay and glaze before they started. They called it a deposit.

### Scene 4 (30 words)
Mrs. Torres paid first. She ordered six ornaments for thirty-six dollars and paid eighteen now. Layla wrote it all down on a form. "This is our pre-order system," she said proudly.

### Scene 5 (30 words)
Riley had to make fourteen ornaments. One per hour meant fourteen hours. That was way too long. "There has to be a faster way," she said, staring at the clay on her wheel.

### Scene 6 (30 words)
Riley tried making seven at once. She shaped all seven, glazed all seven, then fired all seven together. It took four hours, not seven. Making them in a group was much faster!

### Scene 7 (30 words)
She made the next seven the same way. Four more hours. Fourteen ornaments in eight hours instead of fourteen. "I made them in groups!" said Riley. "That saved so much time!"

### Scene 8 (30 words)
"I designed it once," said Riley. "That was the fun part. Then I made them all together. That was the fast part." She could be creative and fast at the same time.

### Scene 9 (30 words)
Ellen called. "All six mugs sold! Can I get eight more?" The café was helping them sell even more during the holidays. Riley started a new batch of mugs right away.

### Scene 10 (30 words)
The second week was the busiest ever. They made one hundred and two dollars. The board was full. Benny's card was red. Nine people were on the waitlist!

### Scene 11 (30 words)
"Pre-order now and yours will be ready by the twenty-second," Benny told a customer. She paid her deposit right away. Waiting made people feel the ornaments were special. Benny liked that.

### Scene 12 (30 words)
Ellen wanted twelve more mugs. The biggest order yet! Riley made another batch. Ellis built racks. Everyone worked from morning to evening. The fort was busy and loud every single day.

### Scene 13 (30 words)
Week three brought in one hundred and eighteen dollars. Everyone worked extra hours. Riley made batch after batch. Ellis sawed boards. Benny ran deliveries. Layla tracked every dollar. Nobody rested until the work was done.

### Scene 14 (30 words)
After the twenty-third, things slowed down. People had their gifts. They made eighty-nine dollars that last week. The team could finally rest. "We made it," said Ellis with a tired smile.

### Scene 15 (30 words)
They counted the whole month. Three hundred and ninety-six dollars! Costs were one hundred and fifty-three. Profit was two hundred and forty-three. The biggest month ever! Everyone stared at the money.

### Scene 16 (30 words)
"We should save some for January," said Layla. "It will be slow." Benny did not want to. "But we earned it!" Layla stayed firm. "We need to save."

### Scene 17 (30 words)
They voted. Three to one. Layla put seventy-three dollars in a jar marked RESERVE. That left one hundred and seventy to split. "Still a lot!" said Riley with a big smile.

### Scene 18 (30 words)
Each kid got forty-two dollars and fifty cents. Layla saved hers. Ellis bought new tools. Riley bought glazes. Benny bought treats and saved the rest. Four kids, four different choices.

### Scene 19 (30 words)
Frances came by. "You saved seeds," she said. "That was smart. Winter is coming." They knew what she meant. The busy time would end and they would need their savings.

### Scene 20 (30 words)
The map glowed bright and golden. Lines went everywhere. But some turned orange. Layla noticed. "This pace will not last," she said. They were glad they saved for what came next.

1. **deposit** (Scene 3) — Money you pay before something is made, to help pay for supplies
2. **batch production** (Scene 6) — Making a bunch of the same thing together to save time
