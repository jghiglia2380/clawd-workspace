# Season 3, Chapter 1: The Invitation Arrives
## Tier 3 Script (Target: 1,300 words, MSL 10-12)

**Scene 1** (68 words)
The friends gathered in their secret fort after school on Friday afternoon. Afternoon sunlight streamed through the wooden windows. Layla, Riley, Ellis, and Benny loved this cozy space. They had built it together last year. Today felt different somehow. The air seemed full of possibilities they couldn't quite name. They didn't know it yet, but something special was about to happen that would change everything.

**Scene 2** (66 words)
Riley noticed something unusual on their table. Three envelopes sat there glowing in the afternoon light. "I didn't put those there," she said with surprise. The friends gathered around to look closer. Each envelope was sealed with colored wax. The seals looked important and mysterious. Nobody knew where they came from or how they got there without anyone noticing.

**Scene 3** (70 words)
Ellis examined the envelopes carefully without touching them yet. "Look at the wax seals," he said. "They're all different from each other." One seal showed a tree shape in green wax. Another showed a clay pot in brown wax. The third showed a gear in silver wax. Each symbol looked like it meant something important. The friends wondered what the symbols represented and who sent them.

**Scene 4** (67 words)
"Should we open them now?" asked Benny with excitement. His green scarf bounced as he leaned forward eagerly. Layla picked up the first envelope with the tree seal. The paper felt thick and special in her hands. "There's something written on the front," she said. It read in beautiful writing: "For the makers and creators of tomorrow." That sounded important!

**Scene 5** (69 words)
Layla carefully broke the green wax seal. She pulled out a card inside. The message was written in beautiful handwriting that looked old. She read it out loud for everyone to hear: "You are invited to learn the ancient art of woodworking. Come to the Grove this Saturday at sunrise. Bring curious hands and an open mind. Signed, Master Woodworker."

**Scene 6** (68 words)
Riley opened the envelope with the clay pot seal next. Her hands trembled slightly with growing excitement. "Mine says something different," she announced to her friends. She read aloud: "You are invited to discover the magic of pottery. Visit the Valley next weekend. Bring patient hearts and creative spirits. Signed, The Potter." The friends looked at each other with wonder and curiosity.

**Scene 7** (70 words)
Ellis picked up the third envelope with the gear symbol. He opened it carefully and read the message inside: "You are invited to explore the world of invention. Journey to the Tower in two weeks. Bring problem-solving minds and brave ideas. Signed, Maestro Gearsmith." Three invitations meant three different crafts to learn. Three different teachers waiting for them. It felt like destiny calling them forward.

**Scene 8** (67 words)
The friends sat quietly for a moment, thinking about the invitations. "Who sent these to us?" wondered Riley. "And how did they know about our fort?" Ellis studied the envelopes again closely. "Look at the seals more carefully," he said. "These were made by hand. Whoever sent them is a real craftsperson. These aren't printed or fake."

**Scene 9** (69 words)
Benny couldn't contain his excitement anymore at all. "This is so amazing!" he said, jumping up quickly. "We get to learn how to make real things!" Layla nodded thoughtfully, her yellow bow bobbing. "But we need to figure out where these places are," she said. "The Grove, the Valley, and the Tower. Does anyone know where those places are located?"

**Scene 10** (68 words)
Ellis pulled out his neighborhood map from his backpack. He always kept it organized and ready for adventures. "Let me check the map," he said, spreading it on the table. The three envelopes sat next to the map. As Ellis studied the paper carefully, something magical happened. The wax seals began to glow much brighter than before. Everyone gasped at once.

**Scene 11** (70 words)
The glowing seals projected light onto the map like tiny lanterns. Three spots on the map lit up with the same glow. "Whoa!" exclaimed Benny loudly. The tree symbol pointed to a place called Willow Grove. The pot symbol showed Potter's Valley to the south. The gear indicated Inventor's Ridge north of town. The map was showing them the way forward. Magic was real!

**Scene 12** (67 words)
"The invitations are showing us the way," Riley said with amazement. She loved when things felt magical and special. Layla grabbed her notebook to write down the locations. "Saturday at sunrise for woodworking," she said, making careful notes. "Then pottery the next weekend. And invention the week after that. We can actually do all three of these amazing things!"

**Scene 13** (69 words)
Ellis calculated the distances on his map carefully. "Willow Grove is about an hour's walk from here," he reported. "We'll need to leave really early Saturday morning. Maybe before dawn." Benny groaned playfully. "Sunrise means waking up when it's still dark outside!" But his eyes sparkled with excitement despite the complaint. "Though it'll be worth it for sure!"

**Scene 14** (68 words)
The friends started planning their first adventure together. "We should bring supplies," suggested Riley. "Maybe sketchbooks to draw what we learn?" Ellis added: "And water bottles for the long walk." Layla organized their ideas into a neat list. Each friend would bring something useful for the journey. They were becoming a well-coordinated team through all their adventures together this summer.

**Scene 15** (70 words)
Riley picked up the woodworking invitation again and reread it. "What does 'curious hands' mean exactly?" she wondered aloud. Ellis thought about it carefully before answering. "I think it means hands that want to touch things. Hands that explore things and learn by doing," he explained thoughtfully. "Not just reading about it in books. Actually making things with our own hands and learning through experience."

**Scene 16** (67 words)
"That's what craftspeople do," Layla said with understanding. "They don't just think about things in their heads. They create them with their hands." She remembered their market idea from earlier adventures. "If we learn these crafts, we could make things. We could share them at a market someday. That would help people and help us learn important skills too."

**Scene 17** (69 words)
The afternoon sun was getting lower through the windows. Long shadows stretched across their table and floor. Benny carefully gathered the three invitations together. He placed them in a special spot on their shelf. "These are really important," he said seriously. "They're not just paper and wax. They're opportunities to learn and grow. Real chances to become makers ourselves." Everyone agreed completely.

**Scene 18** (68 words)
As they prepared to leave the fort for the day, Riley stopped. "What if we're not good at woodworking?" she asked quietly. Sometimes trying new things felt scary and uncomfortable. Layla put a hand on her friend's shoulder. "Then we'll learn," she said confidently and kindly. "That's why they're teaching us these skills. Nobody starts out being perfect at anything new."

**Scene 19** (70 words)
Ellis added his own encouragement to help Riley feel better. "Remember when we first tried organizing that garage?" he reminded them. "We weren't good at it at first at all. But we learned and got better with practice." Benny nodded in agreement. "And these master makers want to teach us! That means they think we can learn. We just have to try our best and stay open."

**Scene 20** (67 words)
The friends locked up the fort and started walking home. The three invitations had changed everything about their summer. Tomorrow they would prepare their supplies. Saturday they would wake before sunrise for the first time. And then they would begin learning skills that people practiced for hundreds of years. "Here's to new adventures," Layla said. Everyone smiled brightly.
