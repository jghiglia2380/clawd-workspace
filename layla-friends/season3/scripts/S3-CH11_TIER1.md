## THE CREATION WORKSHOP

### Scene 1 (32 words)
The friends arrived at the bright community workshop. Sunlight came through the big windows. There were workstations everywhere. One had wood. One had clay. One had mechanical parts. It was perfect for making things!

### Scene 2 (33 words)
Layla put her notes on the table. "We asked people what they wanted to buy," she said. "Most people wanted key hooks and plant holders." The friends looked at the list. They could make those things!

### Scene 3 (32 words)
Ellis read the list carefully. "Key hooks were the most popular," he said. "And small plant holders came next." Riley smiled. "We learned how to make both!" Their training was going to help them now.

### Scene 4 (33 words)
"We need a plan," said Layla. She got out paper. "How many of each should we make?" Benny counted on his fingers. "At least ten of each?" That sounded like a lot. But they could do it!

### Scene 5 (32 words)
"Let us divide the work," said Ellis. "I can do woodworking for the key hooks." Benny jumped up. "I will help with wood too!" They were good at using tools. They had practiced a lot.

### Scene 6 (33 words)
Riley looked at the clay station. "I can make plant holders," she said. Layla nodded. "I will help with pottery too." Then Layla had an idea. "What if we combine wood and clay together?"

### Scene 7 (32 words)
"A combination?" asked Benny. Layla explained. "A wooden stand with a clay pot. Both crafts together!" Ellis thought about it. "That would make something special. Nobody else would have that." Everyone liked this idea!

### Scene 8 (33 words)
The friends split up to work. Ellis and Benny went to the wood station. They got out the tools. Ellis measured the wood carefully. Benny held it steady. Teamwork made the work easier and better!

### Scene 9 (32 words)
Riley and Layla went to the pottery station. They got out the clay. Riley showed Layla how to center it. "Remember what we learned," Riley said. "Start slow. Be patient." Layla tried it carefully.

### Scene 10 (33 words)
Ellis cut the wood for a key hook. He checked his measurements twice. "Measure twice, cut once," he said. That was what Mr. Harrison taught them. Benny sanded the edges smooth. The wood felt nice now.

### Scene 11 (32 words)
Riley shaped a small pot on the wheel. She made the walls even. "This will hold a plant," she said. Layla made one too. Hers was a little wobbly. "Keep trying," Riley said kindly.

### Scene 12 (33 words)
Benny came over to see the pots. "Those look great!" he said. Then he had a question. "Can you make one that fits on my wooden stand?" Riley smiled. "I can try!" Collaboration was helping them create.

### Scene 13 (32 words)
Ellis checked his work for quality. The hook was smooth. The finish was even. "This is good enough to sell," he said proudly. Quality control was important. They learned that from Mrs. Chen at the pottery studio.

### Scene 14 (33 words)
Layla struggled with her pot. It kept leaning to one side. Riley came over. "Put your hands here," she said, showing her. "Now press gently." Layla tried again. This time it worked much better!

### Scene 15 (32 words)
Ellis helped Benny drill holes for the hooks. "Hold it steady," Ellis said. Benny held the wood tight. Ellis drilled carefully. The hole was perfect. They made a good team. They each had different strengths.

### Scene 16 (33 words)
The friends worked all morning. Soon they had finished pieces ready. Five key hooks were done. Six plant pots were drying. And three combination pieces combined both wood and clay. They had made a lot!

### Scene 17 (32 words)
"Let us look at what we made," said Layla. They put everything on one big table. The quality was good. Each piece was made with care. They used all their training from the masters.

### Scene 18 (33 words)
Ellis counted the finished items. "We made fourteen things today," he said. "That is enough for the Makers' Faire!" Benny grinned. "And people actually want to buy these. We did our research!" Planning ahead had worked.

### Scene 19 (32 words)
Riley looked at the combination pieces. "These are my favorite," she said. "We could not make these alone. We needed all our different skills together." Layla agreed. Collaboration created something new and special.

### Scene 20 (33 words)
The friends cleaned up the workshop together. They felt proud and tired. "We learned three crafts," said Layla. "And now we made real things to sell. Our skills combined make us stronger!" Everyone agreed. Teamwork was powerful.
