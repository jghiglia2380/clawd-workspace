## THE INVENTOR'S TOWER

### Scene 1 (32 words)
Two weeks after pottery lessons, the friends met at their fort. On the table sat the third invitation. The gear seal glowed softly. "Today we visit the Gearsmith!" said Layla. Everyone felt excited and curious.

### Scene 2 (33 words)
They followed the map to a tall stone tower. Gears turned on the outside walls. Pulleys moved up and down. "It looks alive!" said Riley. Smoke puffed from the chimney. The tower hummed with mysterious sounds.

### Scene 3 (32 words)
A door opened by itself. Inside, they saw amazing things everywhere! Gears covered the walls. Pulleys hung from the ceiling. Brass machines sat on tables. Blueprints covered every surface. It looked like magic and science together.

### Scene 4 (33 words)
A person popped up from behind a big table. "Welcome, makers!" Maestro Gearsmith wore goggles and had wild gray hair. "I've been expecting you! Are you ready to learn about invention?" Maestro Gearsmith smiled widely at them.

### Scene 5 (32 words)
Maestro Gearsmith showed them strange machines. "Inventions solve problems," said the Gearsmith. "See this?" Maestro Gearsmith pulled a lever. A machine poured water into cups automatically. "This solves the problem of thirsty inventors working!"

### Scene 6 (33 words)
Ellis looked at the machine closely. "How does it work?" he asked. Maestro Gearsmith grinned. "That is the right question! Inventors always ask how things work. Then they make things work better. Questions lead to inventions."

### Scene 7 (32 words)
Maestro Gearsmith took them to a drafting table. Blueprints covered it. "Every invention starts with a drawing," explained the Gearsmith. "First you imagine it. Then you draw it. Then you build it. That is the process."

### Scene 8 (33 words)
Riley saw a shelf full of broken things. "What are those?" she asked. "Those are my failures!" said the Gearsmith cheerfully. "Inventors fail many times before succeeding. Failing teaches us what does not work. Then we try again."

### Scene 9 (32 words)
Benny touched a spinning gear wheel. "Can we make something?" he asked eagerly. "Of course!" said the Gearsmith. "Today you will design your first inventions! Think about problems you want to solve. What would help people?"

### Scene 10 (33 words)
The friends thought hard. Layla said, "Sometimes I lose my pencil." Ellis said, "I drop things when my hands are full." Riley said, "My sketchbook gets wet in rain." Benny said, "I can never find my scarf!"

### Scene 11 (32 words)
"Perfect problems!" said the Gearsmith. "Now we design solutions!" Maestro Gearsmith gave each friend paper and a pencil. "Draw what you imagine. Don't worry about perfect. Just draw your idea. Imagination comes first, always."

### Scene 12 (33 words)
Layla drew a pencil with a string attached to her notebook. Ellis drew a tray with handles. Riley drew a cover for her sketchbook. Benny drew a scarf with clips. Their drawings were simple but showed their ideas.

### Scene 13 (32 words)
Maestro Gearsmith looked at each drawing carefully. "These are wonderful first designs!" said the Gearsmith. "But designs change. We test, we learn, we improve. That is called iteration. We make things better through trying and changing."

### Scene 14 (33 words)
"Let's build a prototype," said the Gearsmith. "A prototype is a first try at making your idea real." Maestro Gearsmith brought boxes of supplies. There were strings, clips, paper, cardboard, and tape. Simple materials for testing ideas.

### Scene 15 (32 words)
The friends started building. Layla tied string from her pencil to her notebook. But the string was too long. "Make it shorter," suggested the Gearsmith. "Testing shows what to change. That is how we learn!"

### Scene 16 (33 words)
Ellis made a cardboard tray. But it bent when he put things on it. "Try making it stronger," said the Gearsmith. Ellis folded the edges. Now it worked better! "You just improved your design through testing!"

### Scene 17 (32 words)
Riley made a paper cover for her sketchbook. "Will it keep water out?" asked the Gearsmith. They tested it with water. The paper got wet. "What material would work better?" Riley thought about it carefully.

### Scene 18 (33 words)
Benny clipped his scarf to his jacket. But the clip kept falling off. "The problem tells you the solution," said the Gearsmith. "What would hold better than one clip?" Benny added more clips. Now it stayed!

### Scene 19 (32 words)
"You are thinking like inventors!" said the Gearsmith proudly. "You found problems. You imagined solutions. You built prototypes. You tested them. You made improvements. This is design thinking! This creates value for people."

### Scene 20 (33 words)
As they left the tower, the friends felt different. "We can invent things!" said Benny. "We can solve problems!" said Ellis. They learned that creativity makes useful things. Ideas can help people. That felt powerful and exciting!

## FL VOCABULARY

- **invention** — Something new that someone creates to solve a problem
- **problem-solving** — Finding ways to fix things that don't work well
- **design** — Planning how something will look and work before making it
- **prototype** — A first try at making an idea real to test it
