## THE INVITATION ARRIVES

### Scene 1 (52 words)
The friends gathered in their secret fort after school on Friday. Afternoon sunlight streamed through the wooden windows. Layla, Riley, Ellis, and Benny loved this cozy space they had built together. Today felt different somehow. The air seemed full of possibilities. They didn't know it yet, but something special was about to happen.

### Scene 2 (53 words)
Riley noticed something unusual on their wooden table. Three envelopes sat there glowing softly in the afternoon light. "I didn't put those there," she said with surprise. The friends gathered around the table to look closer. Each envelope was sealed with colored wax. Nobody knew where they came from or how they got there.

### Scene 3 (52 words)
Ellis examined the envelopes carefully without touching them. "Look at the wax seals," he said. "They're all different." One seal showed a tree shape pressed into green wax. Another showed a clay pot in brown wax. The third showed a gear in silver wax. Each symbol looked like it meant something important.

### Scene 4 (53 words)
"Should we open them?" asked Benny with excitement. His green plaid scarf bounced as he leaned forward. Layla picked up the first envelope with the tree seal. The paper felt thick and special. "There's something written on the front," she said. It read: "For the makers and creators of tomorrow."

### Scene 5 (52 words)
Layla carefully broke the green wax seal and pulled out a card. The message was written in beautiful handwriting. She read it out loud: "You are invited to learn the ancient art of woodworking. Come to the Grove this Saturday at sunrise. Bring curious hands and an open mind. —Master Woodworker"

### Scene 6 (53 words)
Riley opened the envelope with the clay pot seal next. Her hands trembled slightly with excitement. "Mine says something different," she announced. She read: "You are invited to discover the magic of pottery. Visit the Valley next weekend. Bring patient hearts and creative spirits. —The Potter." The friends looked at each other with wonder.

### Scene 7 (52 words)
Ellis picked up the third envelope with the gear symbol. He opened it carefully and read the message inside: "You are invited to explore the world of invention. Journey to the Tower in two weeks. Bring problem-solving minds and brave ideas. —Maestro Gearsmith." Three invitations. Three different crafts to learn together.

### Scene 8 (53 words)
The friends sat quietly for a moment, thinking about the mysterious invitations. "Who sent these?" wondered Riley. "And how did they know about our fort?" Ellis studied the envelopes again. "Look at the seals more closely," he said. "These were made by hand. Whoever sent them is definitely a real craftsperson."

### Scene 9 (52 words)
Benny couldn't contain his excitement anymore. "This is amazing!" he said, jumping up. "We get to learn how to make real things!" Layla nodded, her yellow bow bobbing. "But we need to figure out where these places are," she said practically. "The Grove, the Valley, and the Tower. Anyone know these places?"

### Scene 10 (53 words)
Ellis pulled out his neighborhood map from his backpack. He always kept it organized and ready. "Let me check," he said, spreading the map on the table. The three envelopes sat next to the map. As Ellis studied the paper, something magical happened. The wax seals began to glow brighter than before.

### Scene 11 (52 words)
The glowing seals projected light onto the map like tiny lanterns. Three spots on the map lit up with the same glow. "Whoa!" exclaimed Benny. The tree symbol pointed to a place called Willow Grove on the east side. The pot symbol showed Potter's Valley to the south. The gear indicated Inventor's Ridge north of town.

### Scene 12 (53 words)
"The invitations are showing us the way," Riley said with amazement. She loved when things felt magical and special. Layla grabbed her notebook to write down the locations. "Saturday at sunrise for woodworking," she said, making notes. "Then pottery the next weekend. And invention the week after that. We can do all three!"

### Scene 13 (52 words)
Ellis calculated the distances on his map. "Willow Grove is about an hour's walk from here," he reported. "We'll need to leave really early Saturday morning." Benny groaned playfully. "Sunrise? That means waking up when it's still dark!" But his eyes sparkled with excitement. "Though it'll definitely be worth it!"

### Scene 14 (53 words)
The friends started planning their first adventure together. "We should bring supplies," suggested Riley. "Maybe sketchbooks to draw what we learn?" Ellis added: "And water bottles for the walk." Layla organized their ideas into a list. Each friend would bring something useful. They were becoming a well-coordinated team through all their adventures together.

### Scene 15 (52 words)
Riley picked up the woodworking invitation again and reread it carefully. "What does 'curious hands' mean?" she wondered aloud. Ellis thought about it. "I think it means hands that want to touch things, explore things, and learn by doing," he explained. "Not just reading about it, but actually making things with our own hands."

### Scene 16 (53 words)
"That's what craftspeople do," Layla said with understanding. "They don't just think about things. They create them." She remembered their market idea from earlier adventures. "If we learn these crafts, we could make things to share at a market someday." The connection between learning skills and creating value was becoming clearer to everyone.

### Scene 17 (52 words)
The afternoon sun was getting lower through the fort windows. Long shadows stretched across their table. Benny carefully gathered the three invitations and placed them in a special spot on their shelf. "These are important," he said seriously. "They're not just paper. They're opportunities." Everyone agreed. These invitations mattered.

### Scene 18 (53 words)
As they prepared to leave the fort for the day, Riley stopped at the doorway. "What if we're not good at woodworking?" she asked quietly. Sometimes trying new things felt scary. Layla put a hand on her friend's shoulder. "Then we'll learn," she said confidently. "That's why they're teaching us. Nobody starts out being perfect."

### Scene 19 (52 words)
Ellis added his own encouragement. "Remember when we first tried organizing that garage?" he reminded them. "We weren't good at it at first. But we learned and got better." Benny nodded enthusiastically. "And these master makers want to teach us! That means they think we can learn. We just have to try our best."

### Scene 20 (53 words)
The friends locked up the fort and started walking home together. The three invitations had changed everything. Tomorrow they would prepare. Saturday they would wake before sunrise. And then they would begin learning skills that people had practiced for hundreds of years. "Here's to new adventures," Layla said. Everyone smiled. The journey was beginning.
