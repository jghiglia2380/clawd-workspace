## JOURNEY TO THE WOODWORKER'S GROVE

### Scene 1 (32 words)
The friends woke up very early on Saturday. It was still dark outside. They met at the edge of the neighborhood. Everyone had backpacks. They were ready to go. The adventure was starting!

### Scene 2 (33 words)
Ellis looked at his map. The green light still pointed east. "We walk this way," he said. The friends started walking into the woods. Birds were starting to wake up. It was a beautiful morning.

### Scene 3 (32 words)
The path went through tall trees. The sun was coming up. Riley saw pretty things everywhere. Mushrooms grew on logs. Moss covered rocks. "This forest is magical," she said quietly to her friends.

### Scene 4 (33 words)
Benny walked in front. His green scarf bounced as he walked. "I wonder what the woodworker will be like," he said. Layla thought about it. "Maybe very patient," she said. "Like working with wood."

### Scene 5 (32 words)
After walking for a while, the trees got bigger. These trees were very old and very tall. Their branches made a roof overhead. The light was green and gold. Everything felt peaceful and old.

### Scene 6 (33 words)
"We are almost there," said Ellis. He checked his map again. The green light was very bright now. Through the trees, they could see an opening. This must be Willow Grove! They walked faster.

### Scene 7 (32 words)
The friends walked into a beautiful clearing. Big willow trees stood in a circle. Their branches hung down like curtains. In the middle was a workshop. Tools hung from tree branches. Sawdust covered the ground.

### Scene 8 (33 words)
An old man worked at a big table. He had white hair and a leather apron. His hands moved carefully. He did not look up yet. The friends waited quietly. They did not want to interrupt him.

### Scene 9 (32 words)
The man put down his tools. He looked up at them. His eyes were kind. "You came at sunrise," he said with a smile. "I am Master Thomas. Welcome to my workshop."

### Scene 10 (33 words)
Master Thomas walked over to them. He moved slowly and carefully. "You got my invitation," he said. "Each of you wants to create with your hands. This grove has taught woodworking for a very long time."

### Scene 11 (32 words)
The friends said their names one by one. Master Thomas listened carefully. "Names are important," he said. "In woodworking, everything has a name. Each tool. Each wood. Each way of working." He pointed at tools.

### Scene 12 (33 words)
"First, you must know where wood comes from," said Master Thomas. He walked to a big willow tree. He touched the bark. "These trees give us wood when they fall down. We wait for their gift."

### Scene 13 (32 words)
He showed them wood stacked by the workshop. Each piece had a label. "Wood has memory," he explained. "It remembers the rain and sun. When you work with wood, you work with something special."

### Scene 14 (33 words)
He gave Layla a smooth piece of wood. "Feel it," he said. Layla touched it. She felt lines. "Those lines are the grain," said Master Thomas. "The grain shows how the tree grew."

### Scene 15 (32 words)
Master Thomas showed them the tools. Saws hung from one branch. Other tools hung from other branches. "Each tool has a home," he said. "Organization helps you work better." Ellis liked that very much.

### Scene 16 (33 words)
"Woodworking needs patience," said Master Thomas. "You cannot rush wood. If you go slow and pay attention, the wood will tell you what to make." Riley knew that was true for art too.

### Scene 17 (32 words)
Master Thomas picked up a wooden box. "This took three days to make," he said. The friends looked closely. Every part fit perfectly. It was very smooth. Good work takes time!

### Scene 18 (33 words)
"Next week, you will come back and make boxes," Master Thomas said. "Simple boxes. You will learn measuring, cutting, and sanding." The friends were excited! "Today, choose one piece of wood to take home."

### Scene 19 (32 words)
The friends looked at the wood. Layla picked maple. Riley picked oak. Ellis picked pine. Benny picked walnut. Each friend held their wood carefully. They felt how heavy it was. It felt special in their hands.

### Scene 20 (33 words)
Master Thomas walked them to the edge of the grove. "You did well today," he said. "You listened. You showed respect." The sun was up now. "Study your wood this week," he said. The friends left happy.
