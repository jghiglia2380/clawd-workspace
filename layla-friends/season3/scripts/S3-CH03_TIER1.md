## THE SAWDUST CHALLENGE

### Scene 1 (32 words)
The friends went back to Willow Grove on Saturday. Master Thomas was waiting. He had workstations ready. "Today you will make boxes," he said. The friends felt excited and a little bit nervous!

### Scene 2 (33 words)
Master Thomas showed them how to measure wood. "Measure twice, cut once," he said. He showed them how to use a measuring tape and pencil. The friends practiced on scrap pieces. Measuring exactly right was hard!

### Scene 3 (32 words)
Next was cutting. Master Thomas showed them how to hold the saw. "Let the saw do the work," he said. Layla made the first cut. The saw moved off the line. "Watch the line," he said.

### Scene 4 (33 words)
Benny cut too fast. His piece came out crooked. "Slow down," said Master Thomas kindly. "Now you start over." Benny tried again more carefully. His second cut was much straighter. Going slow helped him do better!

### Scene 5 (32 words)
Riley's cut was almost perfect. She moved slowly and carefully. Master Thomas smiled. "You have patience," he said. Ellis had trouble keeping the saw straight. "Practice helps," Master Thomas told him. "Keep trying!"

### Scene 6 (33 words)
After cutting, they learned about sandpaper. "Sanding makes rough wood smooth," Master Thomas explained. He showed them different sandpaper. Rough paper first. Smooth paper last. "Always sand with the grain," he said. They watched him do it.

### Scene 7 (32 words)
The friends started sanding. Sawdust went everywhere! It covered the tables, the floor, and their clothes. Benny sneezed. "Sawdust shows you are working," Master Thomas said with a smile. "Every craftsperson gets covered in sawdust!"

### Scene 8 (33 words)
Layla's arms got tired from sanding. "How long should I sand?" she asked. Master Thomas felt her wood. "Until it feels smooth," he answered. She kept going. Finally, it felt smooth! Her arms were tired but happy.

### Scene 9 (32 words)
Ellis measured his piece. It was too short now! "I sanded too much off," he said sadly. Master Thomas looked at it. "It still works," he said. "Next time, check more while you sand."

### Scene 10 (33 words)
Master Thomas showed them how to glue pieces together. "Corners must fit right," he said. He showed them how to put on glue. "Not too much. Not too little." They practiced. Getting it right took time.

### Scene 11 (32 words)
Riley's first corner had a gap. "Don't worry," said Master Thomas. "Take it apart and try again." She did. The second time was better! Mistakes help you learn. That felt good to know.

### Scene 12 (33 words)
Benny pushed too hard. Glue squished out everywhere! It got on the table and his hands. "Use less force," Master Thomas said calmly. He helped Benny clean up. Benny's next try was much cleaner!

### Scene 13 (32 words)
While the glue dried, they practiced more cutting. "Practice helps you get better," Master Thomas said. Each friend cut more practice pieces. Each cut was a little bit better than the last one. They could feel themselves improving!

### Scene 14 (33 words)
After drying, they sanded the corners smooth. Master Thomas looked at each box. He found small problems in all of them. "These mistakes show me what to teach you next," he said. "Mistakes help us learn!"

### Scene 15 (32 words)
The friends looked at their boxes. None were perfect. But they were real things they made! "These are your first pieces," Master Thomas said. "Keep them always. They show where you started."

### Scene 16 (33 words)
Master Thomas had them clean up. "Craftspeople take care of their space," he said. They swept the sawdust. They wiped the glue. They put tools away. Cleaning was part of making things too. It showed respect.

### Scene 17 (32 words)
Master Thomas gathered everyone together. "Today you learned important things," he said. "Quality takes time. Mistakes teach you. Patience makes better work than rushing." The friends nodded. They understood this was about more than boxes.

### Scene 18 (33 words)
He showed them a beautiful box from his shelf. "I have worked with wood for fifty years," he said. "I still make mistakes sometimes. I still learn new things. Learning never stops!" That made them feel better.

### Scene 19 (32 words)
"Next week, you will practice again," Master Thomas said. "Same boxes. Same skills. But your hands will remember today." The friends picked up their boxes carefully. They felt proud of what they had made!

### Scene 20 (33 words)
Walking home, the friends talked about their mistakes. "I used too much glue," said Benny. "Mine is too short," said Ellis. But they all felt proud. They had made something real! That was what mattered most.
