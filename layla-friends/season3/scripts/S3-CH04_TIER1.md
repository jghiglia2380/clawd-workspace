## BUILDING BOXES

### Scene 1 (32 words)
The friends went back to the grove for the third week. They brought their old boxes to compare. "You will make new boxes today," said Master Thomas. "Your hands know more now."

### Scene 2 (33 words)
They started with new wood. "Measuring is easier now," Master Thomas said. Layla noticed he was right! Her hands knew what to do. The pencil marks were straighter. Practice was helping her learn without thinking!

### Scene 3 (32 words)
Ellis measured his pieces twice. Both measurements were the same! Last week, he had to measure many times. "Your hands are learning," said Master Thomas. Practice was making him better at measuring wood pieces!

### Scene 4 (33 words)
Cutting was easier this week. Riley's saw stayed on the line. Benny went slowly and his cut was straight. "See how practice helps?" said Master Thomas with a smile. Same people, better skills from practice!

### Scene 5 (32 words)
Sanding went faster too. Not because they rushed. Because they understood how to do it better. Layla's arms did not get as tired. "Good technique makes work easier," explained Master Thomas kindly.

### Scene 6 (33 words)
Master Thomas showed them something new: carving designs! "Now you can add patterns if you want," he said. He showed them simple shapes. Stars, waves, flowers. "Details show you care about your work."

### Scene 7 (32 words)
Riley carved a small flower on her box lid. Master Thomas gave her a carving tool. "Carve away from your body," he said. Riley worked slowly. A flower shape appeared in the smooth wood!

### Scene 8 (33 words)
Ellis carved a star pattern. Each point had to be the same size. He measured with a ruler first. "Plan before you carve," said Master Thomas. "Measure, mark, then carve. Think before you do!"

### Scene 9 (32 words)
Benny carved a wave pattern. It was simpler than a star. "Good choice," Master Thomas said. "Pick the right challenge for your skill." Benny's waves were a bit wobbly but he tried something new!

### Scene 10 (33 words)
Layla did not carve her box. "I want to make the box better first," she said. Master Thomas nodded. "That is wise," he said. "Master the basics first." Sometimes not adding is the right choice.

### Scene 11 (32 words)
When gluing, everyone used just the right amount. Not too much. Not too little. "Your hands remember," said Master Thomas. The corners fit together better too. They had learned from last week's mistakes!

### Scene 12 (33 words)
They compared old boxes with new boxes. The difference was big! New boxes had straighter cuts and smoother wood. "Same students, one week of practice," Master Thomas said. "Practice makes you better." They could see it!

### Scene 13 (32 words)
Riley looked at both her boxes. Last week's box had gaps. This week's box was smooth all over. The flower made it pretty too. "I made both!" she said. "This one is much better!"

### Scene 14 (33 words)
"You got better for three reasons," Master Thomas explained. "First, your hands practiced. Second, you learned from mistakes. Third, you tried again. All three help you grow." The friends listened carefully to this.

### Scene 15 (32 words)
Master Thomas showed them how to put oil on the wood. He rubbed oil on with a cloth. The wood looked more beautiful! "This protects your work," he said while showing them how to do it.

### Scene 16 (33 words)
Each friend put oil on their box. As they rubbed, the wood changed! Hidden patterns appeared. The color got richer and deeper. "The wood thanks you," Master Thomas said. The boxes looked beautiful now!

### Scene 17 (32 words)
They put their boxes on the table. Four boxes sat there. One from week one. One from today. Some with carvings. All showed they were getting better. "This is growth," said Master Thomas.

### Scene 18 (33 words)
"Next week, you learn pottery," Master Thomas said. The friends had almost forgotten! "But you will come back here someday," he promised. "Skills wait for you. They do not disappear when you learn new things."

### Scene 19 (32 words)
The friends thanked Master Thomas. "You taught us about patience and practice," said Layla. Master Thomas smiled. "Those lessons work for everything," he said. "Not just for making wood boxes." That was true!

### Scene 20 (33 words)
Walking home, they carried their boxes carefully. The boxes showed three weeks of learning and getting better. "I am excited for pottery!" said Riley. "But I will miss this place." They all agreed completely.
