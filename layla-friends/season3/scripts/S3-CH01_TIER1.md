## THE INVITATION ARRIVES

### Scene 1 (32 words)
The friends went to their secret fort after school. The sun was shining through the windows. Layla, Riley, Ellis, and Benny loved their fort. Today felt special. Something good was about to happen.

### Scene 2 (33 words)
Riley saw three envelopes on the table. "I did not put those there," she said. The friends looked at the envelopes. Each one had colored wax on it. Where did they come from?

### Scene 3 (32 words)
Ellis looked at the envelopes. "The wax is different on each one," he said. One had a tree. One had a pot. One had a gear. Each symbol looked important.

### Scene 4 (33 words)
"Can we open them?" asked Benny. He was very excited. Layla picked up the first envelope. The paper felt thick. It said: "For the makers and creators of tomorrow." That sounded special!

### Scene 5 (32 words)
Layla opened the green seal. She pulled out a card. She read it out loud. "You are invited to learn woodworking. Come Saturday at sunrise." It was from a Master Woodworker!

### Scene 6 (33 words)
Riley opened the second envelope. It had the pot seal. She read: "You are invited to learn pottery. Come next weekend." It was from The Potter. The friends were amazed by these invitations!

### Scene 7 (32 words)
Ellis opened the third envelope. It had the gear seal. He read: "You are invited to learn about invention. Come in two weeks." It was from Maestro Gearsmith. Three special invitations!

### Scene 8 (33 words)
The friends sat quietly. They thought about the invitations. "Who sent these?" asked Riley. Ellis looked at the seals again. "A real craftsperson made these," he said. "These are made by hand."

### Scene 9 (32 words)
Benny jumped up with excitement. "We get to learn to make things!" he said. Layla nodded. "But we need to find these places," she said. "Where are the Grove, Valley, and Tower?"

### Scene 10 (33 words)
Ellis got his map from his backpack. He put it on the table. The envelopes were next to the map. Then something magical happened. The wax seals started to glow brighter!

### Scene 11 (32 words)
The glowing seals made light on the map. Three spots lit up! The tree showed Willow Grove. The pot showed Potter's Valley. The gear showed Inventor's Ridge. The map was showing them the way!

### Scene 12 (33 words)
"The invitations are magic!" said Riley. She loved magic things. Layla wrote down the locations. "Saturday at sunrise for woodworking," she said. "Then pottery. Then invention. We can do all three!"

### Scene 13 (32 words)
Ellis looked at the map. "Willow Grove is far," he said. "We need to leave early Saturday morning." Benny made a face. "Sunrise is so early!" But he was still excited to go.

### Scene 14 (33 words)
The friends made a plan. "We should bring supplies," said Riley. "Like sketchbooks?" Ellis said, "And water bottles." Layla made a list. Each friend would bring something. They were a good team!

### Scene 15 (32 words)
Riley read the invitation again. "What does 'curious hands' mean?" she asked. Ellis thought about it. "It means hands that want to learn by doing things," he said. "By making things."

### Scene 16 (33 words)
"That is what craftspeople do," said Layla. "They make things." She thought about their market idea. "If we learn these crafts, we can make things to share!" That made sense to everyone.

### Scene 17 (32 words)
The sun was getting low. It was almost dinner time. Benny put the invitations on their shelf. "These are important," he said. "They are opportunities." Everyone agreed. These invitations were special.

### Scene 18 (33 words)
Riley stopped at the door. "What if I am not good at woodworking?" she asked quietly. Layla smiled at her. "Then we will learn," she said. "That is why they will teach us."

### Scene 19 (32 words)
"Remember the garage?" asked Ellis. "We were not good at first. But we learned!" Benny nodded. "These master makers will teach us. We just have to try our best!" That felt good.

### Scene 20 (33 words)
The friends walked home together. The invitations had changed everything. Tomorrow they would get ready. Saturday they would wake up early. Then they would learn new skills. "Here's to new adventures!" said Layla.
