Experience joy of shared accomplishment --- ## THREE TIME OPTIONS ### 🕐 20-MINUTE VERSION: "Quick Market Circle" **Best For:** K-1st grade, condensed experience, time-limited **Materials Needed:** - Student-made items (one per student from previous activities: paper ornaments from CH10, drawings from CH11, etc.) - Simple play money ($5 per student) - Circle seating arrangement **Activity Steps:** 1. **Market Setup (3 minutes)** - Arrange students in circle on floor - Each student places their item in front of them - Receive $5 in play money - Explain: "We're having a market! You can buy one thing from a friend." 2. **Market Time (10 minutes)** - Students walk around circle looking at items - Decide what they want to buy - Approach vendor: "I'd like to buy your ___. How much?" - Vendor sets price (teacher supports: "What's fair?") - Simple transaction: exchange money for item - Everyone buys exactly one thing 3. **Sharing Circle (5 minutes)** - Return to circle seats - Each student shows what they bought - "I bought ___ from ___ for $___ because ___" - Celebrate: everyone traded successfully! 4. **Quick Reflection (2 minutes)** - "What did you learn about markets this year?" - "What was your favorite FL lesson?" **Success Indicator:** Students successfully complete a transaction (buy and sell one item each), use money appropriately, and can name one FL concept they learned this season. --- ### 🕑 30-MINUTE VERSION: "Mini Market with Booths" **Best For:** 1st-2nd grade, balanced market experience **Materials Needed:** - Student-made items from season (crafts, drawings, etc.) - Optional: simple baked goods (store-bought cookies to "sell") - Play money ($20 per student) - "Booth" areas (desks grouped or designated floor sections) - Price tags and markers - Simple tracking sheet **Activity Steps:** 1. **Market Setup (8 minutes)** - Divide class into booth vendors and shoppers (will rotate) - **Booth Types:** * Handmade Crafts (items from CH10) * Art Gallery (items from CH11) * Baked Goods (cookies/crackers—discuss cost of goods CH07) * Repair Shop (Diego-style—fix broken toys or sharpen pencils for fee) - Students at booths: * Arrange items * Create price tags (teacher supports: "Remember labor cost!") * Prepare for customers 2. **Market Round 1 (8 minutes)** - Half class shops, half runs booths - Shoppers browse, decide purchases, negotiate - Vendors sell, make change, track sales - Real-time learning: "My cookies aren't selling—should I lower price?" 3. **Rotation (2 minutes)** - Vendors become shoppers, shoppers become vendors - Quick inventory check: "What sold out? What didn't sell?" 4. **Market Round 2 (8 minutes)** - New vendors, new shoppers - Apply learning from round 1 5. **Reflection & Celebration (4 minutes)** - Count revenue (vendors) - Share purchases (shoppers) - Quick discussion: "What FL concept did you use today?" - Celebrate season learning! **Tracking Sheet Format (Simple):** ``` MY MARKET DAY As a VENDOR: Items I sold: ________________ Total money earned: $_____ As a SHOPPER: Items I bought: ______________ Total money spent: $_____ One FL concept I used: _______________ ``` **Success Indicator:** Students successfully operate as both vendor and customer, make pricing decisions referencing season concepts (labor cost, fair trade, cost of goods), and identify connections to previous chapters. --- ### 🕒 45-MINUTE VERSION: "Full Community Market Day" **Best For:** 2nd-4th grade, comprehensive culminating experience **Materials Needed:** - Variety of student-made items from season activities - Market booth setups (tables, signs, decorations) - Play money (varied bills: $1, $5, $10, $20; $50 per student) - Price tags, receipt books, tracking sheets - "Market Manager" badges - Award certificates - Optional: simple refreshments for "tamale booth" (crackers/cookies representing tamales) - Bell or signal for announcements **Activity Steps:** 1. **Pre-Market Preparation (8 minutes)** - Assign roles: * **Vendors** (60% of class): run booths * **Shoppers** (30%): browse and buy * **Market Managers** (10%): help resolve issues, make change, support - **Booth Setup by Category:** * **Handmade Crafts Booth** (CH10 items): beaded jewelry, ornaments, cards * **Art Gallery** (CH11 items): memory art (some for sale, some priceless/display only) * **Garden Produce Stand** (CH09 concept): shared garden items (real veggies if possible, or representations) * **Baked Goods Booth** (CH07 cost of goods): cookies/crackers with cost calculated * **Repair & Service Station** (CH05): fix broken items, sharpen pencils, for fee * **Trading Post** (CH01): trade items without money - Vendors: * Price items (must justify: materials + labor, or fair trade value) * Arrange booth attractively * Create signs * Assign roles (cashier, sales, inventory) 2. **Market Period 1 (12 minutes)** - Open market! Ring bell - Shoppers browse all booths - Make purchasing decisions - Vendors: * Sell items * Negotiate prices * Track inventory * Provide receipts - Market Managers circulate helping - Real market dynamics occur: * Some items sell out fast (demand!) * Perishables get discount as time passes (CH08) * Vendors adjust prices based on demand * Trades happen at Trading Post 3. **Mid-Market Reflection Pause (3 minutes)** - Ring bell for brief pause - Quick announcements: * "Handmade Crafts Booth is sold out! High demand!" * "Garden Produce dropping prices—perishables!" * "Repair Station has a line—service in high demand!" - Market insights create learning moments - Resume shopping 4. **Rotation Break (2 minutes)** - Vendors and shoppers switch roles - Quick inventory: What sold? What didn't? Why? - Price adjustments for round 2 5. **Market Period 2 (12 minutes)** - Reopened market with role rotation - Apply learning from period 1 - New vendors may price differently based on observations - Continued trading, buying, selling 6. **Market Close & Reflection (8 minutes)** - Ring bell: "Market closing!" - Vendors count revenue, complete tracking sheets - Shoppers review purchases - **Whole-Class Discussion:** * "What FL concepts did you use today?" * "What surprised you about running a market?" * "How did you decide what to buy/how to price?" * "What would you do differently next time?" - **Award Ceremony** (celebrating learning, not just profit): * "Best Negotiator" * "Fairest Trader" * "Most Creative Pricing" * "Kindest Vendor" * "Smartest Shopper" * Everyone gets participation certificate! **Comprehensive Tracking Sheet:** ``` COMMUNITY MARKET DAY - FULL TRACKING SHEET Name: ___________________ Date: __________ MY BOOTH: ____________________ INVENTORY & PRICING: Item Price Why this price? Write "Market Report" analyzing what sold well and why - Graph class data: revenue by booth type - Calculate class totals: total transactions, total money exchanged - Create "FL Concept Poster" showing how all 12 chapters connect -
