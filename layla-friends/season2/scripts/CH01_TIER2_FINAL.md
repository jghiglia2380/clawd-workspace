# CHAPTER 1: THE SWAP MEET - TIER 2 (2ND-3RD GRADE)

**Reading Levels:** J-M
**Target Narration Speed:** 90 WPM
**Target Word Count:** 1060 words
**Episode Duration:** ~12 minutes
**Character Focus:** All four friends
**FL Integration:** Fair trade, exchange, value
**Setting:** Neighborhood community center

---

## THE SWAP MEET

### Scene 1
Layla walked down the sunny street on Saturday morning. She noticed a colorful sign posted on the community center wall. The sign had big bright letters that caught her eye. "Swap Meet Today - 10 AM to 2 PM!" it announced. Layla stopped walking and read every single word carefully. This sounded really interesting!

### Scene 2
The sign explained that people bring things they don't use anymore. Then they can take home different things that other people brought. "That's exactly like our Trading Post game!" Layla said with growing excitement. She felt her heart beat faster with anticipation. This would be so much fun to try!

### Scene 3
Layla ran to Riley's house as fast as she could go. She knocked on the door several times eagerly. Riley opened it and looked surprised to see her. "Come quick!" Layla said, catching her breath. "There's a swap meet at the community center today!" Riley's eyes got wide with interest. She grabbed her shoes right away.

### Scene 4
They found Ellis and Benny playing basketball in the driveway. "A swap meet is happening today!" Layla told them. "We can trade our old stuff for new things!" Ellis stopped bouncing the ball and smiled. "That sounds cool! What should we bring?" Benny asked. They all started thinking hard.

### Scene 5
Layla hurried home and looked through her toy box carefully. She found an old puzzle buried at the bottom. She'd done it at least ten times already this year. "Someone else would probably like this puzzle," she thought to herself wisely. "It's just sitting here collecting dust anyway." She picked it up and set it aside for trading.

### Scene 6
Riley went through her art supplies in her bedroom carefully. She found several short colored pencils in a cup on her desk. She also found a sheet of stickers she'd forgotten about completely. "These pencils still work really good," Riley said while examining them closely. "And these stickers are still pretty." She carefully put them all in a cloth bag.

### Scene 7
Ellis chose a book he'd read twice from his bookshelf. He also grabbed a toy car from his closet. He was getting too big for cars now. "Someone younger would love these things," Ellis said thoughtfully. He felt good about giving them to someone who would enjoy them more.

### Scene 8
Benny searched his messy room for things to trade. He found a bouncy ball under his bed. He also found a toy drum he'd forgotten he owned! "I never play with these anymore," Benny said. He added both items to a backpack. They would make another kid happy.

### Scene 9
The four friends walked together to the community center. They each carried their items to trade carefully. The warm sun felt nice on their faces. They talked about what new things they might find there. The building was getting closer with each step. They could hardly wait!

### Scene 10
When they walked inside, they saw tables filling the entire big room. People were busy putting their items out on the tables neatly. There were so many cool things everywhere they looked! Books, toys, games, and puzzles covered all the surfaces. Mrs. Patterson stood near the entrance and waved at them happily. "Welcome, everyone!" she called out warmly.

### Scene 11
"The rule here is very simple to understand," Mrs. Patterson explained to the children kindly. "You need to make fair trades with other people. That means both people should feel happy about the exchange." She pointed to an empty table in the corner. "You can put your stuff over there." The friends walked over together carefully.

### Scene 12
They began setting out their trading items on the table. Layla's puzzle went down first in the center. Then Riley placed her pencils and stickers next to it. Ellis added his book and toy car. Finally, Benny put down his ball and drum. "Let's walk around and look!" Layla suggested with a smile.

### Scene 13
The friends walked around the room, looking at everything carefully. They saw old toys on many tables. They saw stacks of books about different topics. They saw board games and card games too. So many interesting things! People were smiling at each other and talking nicely. Everyone seemed really happy.

### Scene 14
Riley suddenly saw something she wanted on a table. It was a set of watercolor paints in a wooden box! An elderly lady sat behind that table. "Would you like to trade for those paints?" the lady asked Riley kindly. Riley nodded her head very fast.

### Scene 15
"What do you have to trade?" the lady asked with interest. Riley showed her the colored pencils from home. The lady picked them up and smiled big. "Oh, my grandkids would love these for art projects!" she said happily. They made the trade right there. Riley held the paint box carefully.

### Scene 16
Later, the friends gathered to show their new things proudly. Riley had her watercolor paint set. Ellis had found a cool magnifying glass. Just then, a young boy walked by them. He was carrying Benny's old drum and banging it loudly. Benny laughed out loud at the sound.

### Scene 17
"I completely forgot about that drum," Benny said, grinning widely. "But look at how happy he is now!" The boy banged the drum again with joy on his face. He loved it very much. Benny felt really good inside. His old toy was making someone new very happy.

### Scene 18
A small boy with curly hair noticed Layla's puzzle on the table. "I want that puzzle!" he said to Layla excitedly. Layla smiled at him in a warm way. She had done that puzzle many times before. But for this boy it would be brand new! They traded happily.

### Scene 19
The swap meet came to an end in the afternoon. The four friends sat on a bench outside together. They looked at their new things with satisfaction. "We didn't spend any money at all," Layla pointed out. "But we still got good stuff!" Everyone agreed with her.

### Scene 20
"Our Trading Post game was pretty small," Layla said thoughtfully while looking around. "But this swap meet brought the whole neighborhood together!" She looked at each of her friends' faces. "What if we made even more events like this?" An idea was growing in her mind. Maybe they could create something bigger!
