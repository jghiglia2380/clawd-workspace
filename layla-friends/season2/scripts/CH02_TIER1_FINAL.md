## THE LEMONADE PROBLEM

### Scene 1
It was a very hot day. Layla sat by her window. She felt so warm! "Everyone must be thirsty," she said. "I should sell lemonade today!" That seemed like a really good idea. She got up right away.

### Scene 2
Layla went to the kitchen. She squeezed lemons into a pitcher. She added sugar next. She poured in cold water. She stirred and stirred it. The lemonade looked really yummy. "This will sell fast!" she said.

### Scene 3
Layla set up a table outside. She put out cups. She made a big sign. It said "LEMONADE 50¢" in big letters. Everything looked nice and ready. Now she needed customers. She sat down to wait.

### Scene 4
Layla looked down the street. She saw another lemonade stand! It was at Riley's house! "Wait," Layla said. "Riley had the same idea?" She felt very surprised. Two stands now!

### Scene 5
Layla walked down the street. She counted more stands as she went. Riley had one lemonade stand. Ellis had one stand too. Benny had one also. Other kids had stands! "Six lemonade stands!" Layla said. "All on one street!"

### Scene 6
Layla went back to her stand. She waited a long time there. People walked by on the sidewalk. But nobody stopped to buy. Nobody wanted her lemonade at all. Layla felt really sad. Her pitcher was still full.

### Scene 7
Layla walked to Riley's stand. "Did you sell any?" Layla asked. Riley shook her head no. "Not one cup," Riley said sadly. They both looked confused. It was so hot!

### Scene 8
Ellis and Benny came over. Nobody had sold anything. "I don't get it," Benny said. "Everyone should be thirsty!" Ellis looked at all the stands. He started thinking hard.

### Scene 9
Ellis counted the stands again. "Six stands," he said. "But not many people walking by. There's too much lemonade!" He was seeing the problem. Too many sellers! Not enough buyers!

### Scene 10
"We're all trying to sell," Ellis said. "But there aren't enough people!" The friends nodded. That made sense to them. Too many stands for buyers! That was the problem. They needed a new plan.

### Scene 11
Another kid changed their sign. The price went down lower. It said 25 cents now. "Look!" said Layla. "They made it cheaper!" Maybe that would help. It seemed smart.

### Scene 12
Soon everyone lowered their prices. 25 cents at one stand. 20 cents at another. 15 cents at the next. The signs kept changing. Everyone wanted to be cheapest. Still nobody bought much!

### Scene 13
"This is really bad," Benny said. "Now lemonade costs almost nothing. And still no one buys!" Riley agreed. "We need a different plan." Making things cheaper wasn't working.

### Scene 14
Layla had a new idea. "What if we work together?" she asked. "One big stand instead of six?" Her friends looked at each other. That might work well! "Let's try it!" said Benny.

### Scene 15
The friends put everything together. They brought all their lemons. They brought all their cups. They made one big table. "We have so much now!" Benny said. This looked better.

### Scene 16
Riley made a pretty new sign. Ellis put cups in rows. Benny set up the table. Layla added lemon slices. Everyone helped with the work. The stand looked really amazing!

### Scene 17
The new stand was nice. People walking by stopped to look. "Fresh lemonade!" the friends called. "Made by friends!" The stand was pretty. People wanted to buy!

### Scene 18
People started buying lemonade! The friends worked as a team. One friend poured lemonade. One friend took the money. It was really fun now! "This is so much better!" Benny said. Everyone was happy. Business was good!

### Scene 19
The sun went down. The friends counted all their coins. They made more money together. Much more than working alone! Ellis smiled. "Together we're special!" Working together was better!

### Scene 20
"For our market plan," Layla said. "We shouldn't all sell the same thing!" The friends agreed. "We need different things!" Everyone brings something different. Then everyone can do well!
