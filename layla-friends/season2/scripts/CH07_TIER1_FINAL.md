## THE ITALIAN BAKERY

### Scene 1
Mr. Mason reached into old trunk again today. He pulled out bread stamp carefully with hands. Patterns carved deep into hard wood nicely. "This from Italy long ago," he said smiling big. "From Rossi family bakery there. Very special place. Taught me lots about costs!"

### Scene 2
"Rossi family owned bakery long long time," Mr. Mason said warmly. "Grandfather taught son everything about baking bread. Son taught daughter Maria all baking secrets. Three whole generations worked there together! Kept old traditions alive every single day!"

### Scene 3
"I stayed small village two weeks," Mr. Mason said happily smiling. "Every morning I smelled fresh bread baking early. Filled whole street warmly with good smells! Antonio invited me watch them bake bread. I was so very excited!"

### Scene 4
"Bakery opened five every morning early," Mr. Mason said to them. "Antonio and Maria already working very hard inside. Mixing heavy dough with strong arms together. Shaping perfect loaves with careful hands. Oven glowed hot orange bright. Made perfect bread every single day!"

### Scene 5
"How much bread cost?" Benny asked curious. "Two euros for each big loaf," Mr. Mason said nodding. "I thought good fair price. Bread smelled so wonderful fresh. Looked golden brown and perfect. Wanted buy some right away that day!"

### Scene 6
"But Antonio explained important thing to me," Mr. Mason said seriously now. "Two euros not all profit money. Must pay for flour first. Yeast for dough too. Salt and water both. Electricity for hot oven. Rent for shop space too. Costs before keeping any money!"

### Scene 7
"Antonio showed me the math," Mr. Mason said. "Flour fifty cents per loaf. Yeast ten cents each. Electricity twenty cents each baking. That eighty cents total! Costs add up fast!"

### Scene 8
"Rent two hundred euros monthly every month," Mr. Mason said to him. "They sold three hundred loaves each month. So seventy cents each for rent only. Plus ingredients costs too. Getting expensive make bread. Must pay everything before any profit!"

### Scene 9
"Add it up," Mr. Mason said. "Eighty cents ingredients. Seventy cents rent. One fifty total! Must pay before selling. Costs before profit comes."

### Scene 10
"Only fifty cents profit?" Layla asked surprised. "Yes only!" Mr. Mason said nodding. "That pays food for family. Housing too. Clothes for them. Everything family needs. Not much but enough. Must sell lots lots bread!"

### Scene 11
"Some people complained," Mr. Mason said. "Said two euros expensive. But didn't understand costs. Antonio explained costs. Most people understood then. Some still complained anyway!"

### Scene 12
"I helped next day morning," Mr. Mason said proud. "Mixed heavy dough many hours! Arms got so tired. So sore after! Very hard work. Antonio smiled big: 'Now you understand real price!' Very very hard work!"

### Scene 13
"What if less?" Riley asked. "If one fifty, no profit," Mr. Mason said. "Can't pay food. Can't live. Bakery closes. Need profit survive. Must charge enough!"

### Scene 14
"What if more?" Benny asked. "Like three euros?" "Customers leave," Mr. Mason said. "Go grocery store. They sell cheaper. Lose all customers quickly!"

### Scene 15
"Two euros right," Mr. Mason said. "High enough stay open. Low enough keep customers. Must balance both. Every business needs right price."

### Scene 16
"Maria showed recipe book," Mr. Mason said. "Passed down generations. Each recipe had cost notes. Knew what cost make. Tracked everything careful!"

### Scene 17
"Family chose quality," Mr. Mason said. "Could use cheap flour. Make faster. But taste bad. Customers notice. Quality matters! People pay for good bread!"

### Scene 18
"For our market," Benny said, "must know costs first. Know what costs make lemonade. Cookies. Everything. Then set fair price. Cover all costs!"

### Scene 19
"Exactly!" Mr. Mason said. "Calculate lemons. Sugar. Cups. Everything cost. Then know minimum. Must charge more. That profit. Keeps going!"

### Scene 20
Mr. Mason held stamp. "Every product has costs. Good business knows costs. Prices fair. Explains value. That builds trust! Remember lesson always!" Friends nodded understanding.
