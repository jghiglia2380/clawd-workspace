## THE MOROCCAN SPICE MARKET

### Scene 1
The friends sat around the trunk. Mr. Mason held a spice bag. It was small and brown. "Ready for a story?" he asked. Everyone nodded yes. They were excited. He opened the bag. Spices from Morocco!

### Scene 2
The smell filled the room! Sweet cinnamon and saffron. Many good smells. "These are from Morocco," Mr. Mason said. "A market far away. Across the ocean." His eyes looked happy. The friends breathed deep. It smelled amazing!

### Scene 3
"Picture colorful stalls," Mr. Mason said. "Narrow streets with awnings. Spices piled high! Bright colors!" Layla imagined it. She could almost see it. "How pretty!" she said. Mr. Mason smiled. "It was amazing!"

### Scene 4
"Merchants called out loud," Mr. Mason said. "Best saffron here! Fresh cumin today! Come buy!" He showed with hands. He acted it out. "Everyone wanted customers. Very busy!" The friends listened close. This sounded exciting!

### Scene 5
"I needed saffron," Mr. Mason said. "I saw a big pile. The merchant smiled. 'How much?' I asked. 'Fifty dirhams,' he said. That's Morocco money." The friends listened. They wanted to know more.

### Scene 6
"Dirhams are Morocco money," Mr. Mason said. "Like our dollars. Fifty seemed a lot! But I didn't know if fair. So I walked away. I thanked him." He acted it out.

### Scene 7
"Wait, wait!" the merchant called. Mr. Mason acted it. He showed them. "Come back! Forty dirhams!" I kept walking. I didn't stop. "Thirty-five!" he shouted. I stopped. I turned around.

### Scene 8
"That taught me something," Mr. Mason said. "The first price isn't always real. Merchants expect you to negotiate. It's like a game!" The friends nodded. They understood now.

### Scene 9
"But how do you know fair?" Layla asked. She was confused. "Great question!" Mr. Mason said. "You look at other stalls. You compare prices. Then you know! Knowledge helps!" Layla nodded happily.

### Scene 10
"I had looked before," Mr. Mason said. "Another person paid thirty. So thirty-five was close. 'Twenty-five,' I said to him. I offered less." The merchant shook his head. Too low! Not enough!

### Scene 11
"He thought hard," Mr. Mason said. "Then smiled big. 'Thirty, plus free cumin!' We shook hands. Both felt happy! A good deal!" The friends smiled. What a good deal!

### Scene 12
"Why not one price?" Riley asked. She didn't understand. "Some markets like negotiating," Mr. Mason said. "It's their culture. It's how they do things. Both people talk together!" Riley nodded. That was interesting.

### Scene 13
"Fair means both happy," Mr. Mason said. "Not just one wins. Both should feel good. That's important! That's the right way!" The friends agreed. That made good sense.

### Scene 14
"What if lots want same thing?" Benny asked. He was thinking. "Good thinking!" Mr. Mason said. "If rare, price goes up. If common, price goes down. That's how it works!" Benny nodded. He understood.

### Scene 15
"That day, saffron was rare," Mr. Mason said. "Bad weather hurt plants. Less saffron meant higher prices. People still bought it. They needed it!" The friends understood the lesson. They were learning!

### Scene 16
"Next day I needed pepper," Mr. Mason said. "Every stall had mountains! Price was very low. Lots of supply! Too much pepper!" He smiled at the good memory. That was lucky!

### Scene 17
Layla thought hard. She was learning. "For our market, know value. Negotiate fairly. Make everyone happy. Both people!" Mr. Mason nodded proud. "You learned the lesson!" he said happily.

### Scene 18
"Exactly right!" Mr. Mason said. "Prices are about value. What people think worth. Not just money! It's more than money!" The friends nodded. They understood it now. This made sense!

### Scene 19
Ellis said, "The merchant could have said thirty first. But negotiating taught you more! You learned better!" Mr. Mason agreed. He nodded. "Yes! I learned how markets really work! I understand now!"

### Scene 20
"For your market," Mr. Mason said, "start with fair prices. Be ready to negotiate. Make both people feel good. That's how markets work! That's the right way!" The friends felt ready! They could do this!
