## THE LEMONADE PROBLEM

### Scene 1
Layla sat by her bedroom window on a scorching summer afternoon. The sun blazed relentlessly in the cloudless sky, making everything shimmer. She fanned herself with an old magazine, but it barely helped. Sweat dripped down her forehead despite staying indoors with the fan running. "Everyone in the neighborhood must be incredibly thirsty today," she thought. Then a brilliant idea struck her mind. A lemonade stand would be perfect for a day like this!

### Scene 2
Layla hurried to the kitchen with building excitement about her plan. She found several fresh lemons in the refrigerator and began squeezing them. The juice poured steadily into her largest glass pitcher as she worked. She added white sugar and ice-cold water from the tap. She stirred everything together until it mixed perfectly and looked just right. The lemonade turned beautifully yellow and wonderfully refreshing. "This is definitely going to sell out fast today!" she said with confidence.

### Scene 3
Layla carefully carried a small folding table from the garage to her yard. She set up clear plastic cups in several neat rows across the table. She placed her full pitcher right in the center of the display. Then she made a big colorful sign with her best markers reading "LEMONADE 50¢". She added lemon drawings around the edges for decoration. Everything looked professional, organized, and ready for serious business she thought. She settled into her chair, waiting for customers to arrive.

### Scene 4
Layla looked up from adjusting her cups and froze in place. She noticed something entirely unexpected happening down the street from her house. A few houses away, someone else was setting up what appeared to be a stand. They were arranging cups and positioning a pitcher on their table. She squinted hard against the bright sunlight to see who it was. "Wait just a minute — is that at Riley's house?" she said in confusion. Riley had the exact same brilliant business idea today!

### Scene 5
Layla stood up and walked slowly down the sidewalk to investigate. Her eyes grew wider with each step she took. Riley's lemonade stand was just the beginning of the problem, she realized. Ellis had set up a lemonade stand at his house on the corner. Benny had one at his house as well, she noticed with growing concern. Several other neighborhood kids had established stands along the block! She counted them all in complete disbelief. Six separate lemonade stands on one single street!

### Scene 6
Layla returned to her own stand feeling confused and worried. She sat there waiting for customers for well over an hour. The hot afternoon sun beat down on her relentlessly from above. People occasionally walked by on the sidewalk in front of her house. But absolutely nobody stopped to buy any lemonade from her stand. They just kept walking past without even glancing at her display. Layla rested her chin in both hands sadly, feeling discouraged. Her pitcher was still completely full of untouched lemonade.

### Scene 7
Layla decided to walk over to Riley's stand to compare experiences. Riley looked just as confused and frustrated as Layla felt inside. Her pitcher was still full too, completely untouched. "Have you managed to sell any lemonade yet today?" Layla asked. She desperately wanted good news from her friend. Riley shook her head slowly with a disappointed expression. "Not even one single cup all afternoon long," Riley admitted sadly. "I really don't understand what's happening — it's so incredibly hot outside!"

### Scene 8
Ellis and Benny came walking over to join them a few minutes later. The four friends gathered together in the middle of the street. Everyone shared the exact same disappointing story about their business today. Plenty of lemonade sitting in their pitchers ready to sell. But absolutely zero customers buying anything from any of them. "I was completely sure everyone would be thirsty on a day like this," Benny said. His voice sounded frustrated and puzzled. Ellis looked carefully up and down the entire street. His analytical mind started working on the problem.

### Scene 9
Ellis studied the situation very carefully for several long moments. He counted all the lemonade stands again from where they stood. Then he started counting people walking by during minutes of observation. The numbers didn't match up well at all, he realized. "Let me think carefully through this problem step by step," he said slowly. His friends watched him think in silence, waiting. "Six stands total here on our street right now. Maybe only twenty people walking by each hour at most. That means about three or four possible customers per stand maximum."

### Scene 10
"I think I finally understand the real problem here," Ellis announced. His three friends all turned to listen carefully to his explanation. "There's way too much lemonade available on our street overall. But there aren't nearly enough people who want to buy it. We're all desperately trying to sell to the exact same small group. The same potential customers walking by on the sidewalk." Layla nodded as his explanation started making sense. They were directly fighting against each other for customers!

### Scene 11
Layla noticed something interesting happening at one of the other stands. One of the other kids was actively changing their price sign. The original price of fifty cents was being crossed out. The new price said just twenty-five cents instead, half the original. "Look at what's happening over there right now," she pointed out. "They're lowering their price to attract more customers to their stand. Instead of ours or anyone else's stand here today." The competitive approach seemed clever at first.

### Scene 12
Soon all the other stands followed the exact same approach. Prices started dropping everywhere almost at once like dominoes falling. Twenty-five cents quickly became twenty cents at one stand. Twenty cents became just fifteen cents at another stand after that. Kids frantically changed their signs over and over with their markers. Each one tried to be the absolute cheapest option available. But even with much lower prices, hardly anyone was buying.

### Scene 13
The four friends gathered back together feeling discouraged. "This is absolutely terrible for all of us," Benny groaned dramatically. He looked down at his nearly full pitcher with frustration. "Now we're practically giving our lemonade away for nothing. And still nobody's actually buying anything from anyone here today!" Riley crossed both her arms across her chest. "There has to be a smarter solution than this approach. We can't keep making everything cheaper forever without success."

### Scene 14
Layla's face suddenly brightened as a new idea formed. Her eyes lit up with renewed hope and excitement. "What if we just stopped fighting against each other entirely?" she suggested. Everyone in the group looked at her with interest and curiosity. "Instead of having six small stands all fighting for customers. We could combine our resources and create ONE amazing stand together!" Her friends considered the possibility carefully. Working together might change everything for the better.

### Scene 15
The four friends started carrying their supplies over to Layla's yard. They combined all their fresh lemons together in one place. They gathered all their pitchers and cups into a single area. Working together they had more than enough supplies for a serious operation. "We can make way more by working together as a team," Ellis observed. He looked at their combined supplies with satisfaction. "And it only takes one good stand to attract attention."

### Scene 16
Each friend contributed their special skills to building the stand. Riley painted a beautiful new sign with bright cheerful colors everywhere. She added fancy decorative letters that looked professional. Ellis carefully organized all the supplies for quick serving of customers. Benny arranged the cups in an impressive pyramid display structure. Layla sliced fresh lemons to float in the pitchers for appeal. The floating lemon slices made everything look fancy and appealing.

### Scene 17
Their newly combined stand looked absolutely amazing compared to before. The large colorful sign caught everyone's attention from far down the street. The neat, organized setup looked professional and trustworthy to customers. People walking by the house actually stopped to look at the display. "Fresh lemonade available here!" the four friends called out together. "Made by friends, served with genuine smiles for everyone!" The positive energy felt completely different from earlier.

### Scene 18
Customers finally started lining up to buy lemonade at long last! Without so much confusing variety cluttering the entire street anymore. Their single impressive stand became the obvious choice for everyone passing. The friends worked together smoothly as an efficient team. One friend poured lemonade carefully into cups for each customer. Another friend collected money efficiently with a smile. A third friend thanked each customer warmly as they left. "This setup is so much better than six separate stands!" said one customer.

### Scene 19
As the sun began setting low in the evening sky. The four friends sat down together on the grass and counted earnings. They had made much more money working together as a team. Much more than they ever would have earned separately. Ellis smiled broadly at the important lesson they had learned. "When there's too much of something available in a market. Prices drop fast and nobody wins in the end. But working together cooperating made us special today!"

### Scene 20
Layla connected the day's lesson to their bigger future plans. Her mind raced ahead with new ideas and realizations. "For our Community Market idea to work well in the future. We definitely shouldn't all sell the exact same things. If everyone brings the exact same items to sell every time. Nobody does well in business there with all that duplication. But if everyone brings something completely different and unique instead. There's no destructive fighting — just wonderful variety for shoppers!" The friends nodded.
