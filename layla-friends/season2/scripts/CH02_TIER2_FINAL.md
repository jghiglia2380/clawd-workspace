## THE LEMONADE PROBLEM

### Scene 1
Layla sat by her bedroom window on the hottest summer day. The sun blazed overhead, making heat shimmer off the sidewalk. She fanned herself with a piece of paper. "Everyone must be thirsty today," she thought with excitement. A lemonade stand would be perfect! She jumped up to start her plan.

### Scene 2
Layla hurried to the kitchen with her brilliant plan. She found fresh lemons in the refrigerator and squeezed them carefully. She added sugar and ice-cold water, stirring everything until it looked right. The lemonade turned bright yellow and refreshing. "This is going to sell fast!" she said happily.

### Scene 3
Layla carried a small folding table out to her front yard. She arranged paper cups in neat rows and placed the pitcher. She created a colorful sign that said "LEMONADE 50¢" with lemon drawings. Everything looked perfect and ready for customers to arrive. Now she just waited for business to start.

### Scene 4
Layla looked up and saw something surprising down the street. Another lemonade stand was being set up by someone she knew. She squinted through the bright sunshine to see better who it was. "Wait a minute — is that Riley?" she said with confusion. Riley had the exact same idea!

### Scene 5
Layla walked down the hot sidewalk to investigate the situation. Her eyes grew wider with each step as she looked around. Riley had a lemonade stand on the corner there. Ellis had a stand at his house too. Benny had a stand as well! Several other kids had set up stands everywhere. She counted six different lemonade stands on one street!

### Scene 6
Layla returned to her stand feeling worried and waited for customers. An entire hour passed slowly under the hot summer sun. A few people walked past, but nobody stopped to buy anything. They looked confused by so many choices available on the street. Layla rested her chin in her hands, staring sadly at nothing.

### Scene 7
Layla walked over to Riley's lemonade stand to check on her. Riley looked just as confused and disappointed as Layla felt. "Have you sold any lemonade yet?" Layla asked hopefully, crossing her fingers. Riley shook her head with frustration showing clearly. "Not even one cup! It's so hot today!"

### Scene 8
Ellis and Benny walked over to join them in the shade. Everyone was having the same frustrating problem with their stands. Lots of delicious lemonade but zero sales for anyone. "I thought everyone would be thirsty today!" Benny said with frustration. Ellis looked around at all the competing stands carefully.

### Scene 9
Ellis counted all the lemonade stands and then counted people walking. "Let me figure this problem out," he said slowly. "Six different stands, but maybe only twenty people passing by today. That's not very many customers for each of us to share." He was starting to understand the real problem.

### Scene 10
"I think I understand what's happening here," Ellis explained to his friends. "There's way too much lemonade available for everyone selling. Not enough people who want to buy it from us. We're all trying to sell to the same small group!" The friends nodded as the idea started making sense.

### Scene 11
Layla noticed another kid changing their price sign to something lower. Fifty cents quickly became twenty-five cents in bright red marker on cardboard. "Look at that stand!" she pointed out to everyone with interest. "They're making it cheaper to get more customers over there." The idea seemed rather clever at first.

### Scene 12
Soon everyone on the street was dropping their prices lower and lower. Twenty-five cents became twenty cents at some stands very quickly now. Then twenty cents became just fifteen cents per cup at others. Signs changed all over the street as kids tried desperately now. But even with lower prices, people still weren't buying much lemonade.

### Scene 13
The four friends gathered together in the shade, feeling frustrated and tired. "This situation is terrible," Benny groaned loudly with disappointment in his voice. "Now we're giving lemonade away, and customers still aren't buying anything!" Riley crossed her arms with determination showing on her face. "There has to be a better idea than this."

### Scene 14
Layla's face suddenly lit up with a new idea that might work. "What if we stopped fighting against each other?" she suggested thoughtfully. "Instead of six small struggling stands, we could make ONE amazing stand!" Her friends thought carefully about it. Working together might change everything for the better!

### Scene 15
The four friends brought all their supplies to Layla's yard for combining. They combined their fresh lemons, their pitchers, and all their paper cups. Together they had way more supplies than any one stand alone. "We can do so much more by working together!" Ellis said. Cooperation was the key to solving this problem today.

### Scene 16
Each friend helped build the new improved stand using their special skills. Riley painted a beautiful colorful sign with amazing artistic details on it. Ellis organized everything for fast and smooth customer service at the counter. Benny arranged the cups in an attractive display that looked great. Layla added lemon slices floating beautifully in each pitcher for style.

### Scene 17
Their combined lemonade stand looked amazing and totally professional compared to before. The big colorful sign caught people's attention from way down the street. The impressive setup looked inviting and well-organized, making people want to stop. "Fresh lemonade!" the friends called out together with big smiles. "Made by friends!" The energy was different now.

### Scene 18
Customers finally started buying lemonade from their impressive stand at last! Without so many stands on the street, their setup became obvious. The friends worked smoothly as a team pouring lemonade and taking money. They thanked each customer warmly as they served them all. "This is better than before!" one satisfied customer said to them.

### Scene 19
The sun started going down as the successful afternoon came to end. The friends counted their money together with excitement and growing happiness inside. They had made way more profit today than they expected at all. They earned more working together than they would have made alone. Ellis smiled at the important lesson learned. "When there's too much, nobody wins."

### Scene 20
Layla thought carefully about their Community Market idea and what they learned. "For our market, we shouldn't all bring the same things," she said. "If everyone sells lemonade, nobody does well at all in business. But if everyone brings something different, there's no fighting over customers!" Variety would be the key to success for their future market.
