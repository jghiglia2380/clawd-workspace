## THE BUSY SATURDAY

### Scene 1
The four friends met outside the community center on Saturday morning. The weather was perfect for spending time outside. They hadn't planned anything special for the day yet. Before they could decide what to do, several neighbors started walking toward them. It looked like everyone in the neighborhood needed help with something today!

### Scene 2
Mrs. Patterson arrived first with a worried look on her face. "My garage is a total mess," she explained with a tired sigh. "Can you help me organize it?" Then Frances walked over from across the street. "My garden needs serious weeding," she said with frustration. Mr. Johnson appeared next carrying a stack of papers. "Could you deliver these flyers around the neighborhood?" he asked. The friends felt overwhelmed.

### Scene 3
"We definitely want to help everyone!" said Layla to her friends. "Let's start with Mrs. Patterson's garage first." They walked to her house together as a group. "If we all work together, we'll finish fast," Layla said with confidence. They crowded into the very messy garage. Boxes and tools were everywhere!

### Scene 4
Inside the garage, things got confusing really quickly. Benny picked up a heavy box to move it. But Ellis was already sorting things from that same box! Riley and Layla both reached for the same lamp at once. Everyone kept bumping into each other in the tight space. "Wait, I was organizing those!" Ellis said with frustration.

### Scene 5
Thirty long minutes went by slowly without much progress at all. Mrs. Patterson came to check on how things were going. The garage looked almost the same as when they started. "How's it going?" she asked kindly. Layla sighed deeply. "We're trying our best, but it's really hard."

### Scene 6
Ellis stopped working and thought about what was happening. "I think we keep getting in each other's way," he said honestly. "What if each person did just ONE job instead of everyone doing everything?" The others stopped to think about his idea. It made a lot of sense.

### Scene 7
They split up the jobs based on everyone's natural strengths. Benny would carry heavy boxes because he was strong and had lots of energy. Ellis would sort and label things because he was organized and liked details. Riley would arrange things because she was creative and artistic. Layla would direct everyone and keep things moving.

### Scene 8
With everyone working in their own role, work started again with new energy. This time felt totally different from before. Benny moved boxes without anyone in his way. Ellis sorted items without being interrupted by others. Riley made the shelves look pretty and neat. Layla kept everything moving forward smoothly.

### Scene 9
The garage completely changed into an organized space that looked amazing. Boxes stood in neat stacks along the walls. Tools hung in organized rows on the pegboards. Clear paths made it easy to walk around safely. "Wow!" said Riley with real amazement. "We actually did it!"

### Scene 10
Mrs. Patterson came back and couldn't believe her eyes when she saw the change. "This is amazing!" she said with genuine happiness in her voice. "You figured out something really important here today. When everyone does what they're best at, the team wins. There's a name for this idea. It's called division of labor."

### Scene 11
Feeling great about their success, the friends headed to Frances's garden next. This time they planned ahead while walking to her house. "Let's decide our jobs BEFORE we start working," Layla suggested wisely to everyone. Everyone agreed right away. Benny would do heavy work. Ellis would organize everything. Riley would plant. Layla would direct.

### Scene 12
In Frances's garden, everyone went straight to their jobs without any confusion. Benny pulled out the big stubborn weeds with his strong arms. Riley planted flowers in beautiful patterns that looked really colorful. Ellis kept track of what was done on paper. Layla helped wherever she was needed most. Frances watched with surprise.

### Scene 13
What could have taken all day was done in just one hour! The garden rows looked neat and colorful with flowers everywhere. New flowers bloomed in pretty patterns across the whole garden. The friends gave each other happy high-fives. "Done already?" Benny laughed. "That would have taken forever before!"

### Scene 14
Next came delivering the flyers around the neighborhood. Ellis looked at a neighborhood map and made the best route. He split up the streets among them. Layla and Riley each took nearby areas. Benny ran to the houses that were farther away. Everyone moved quickly with their deliveries.

### Scene 15
The friends met back at their starting spot. Every single flyer had been delivered to the right houses. Empty bags hung from their arms as proof. The sun was still high in the sky. "All done!" Layla said happily with pride. "And it's not even noon yet!" Three jobs finished already!

### Scene 16
They rested on a front porch together, sharing snacks and cold drinks. The morning had been so productive for their team. "That felt so different once we split up the work," Riley said thoughtfully. Ellis nodded in agreement. "Having my own job meant I could focus without getting confused."

### Scene 17
The friends talked about their different strengths that made them work well together. Benny was strong and full of energy for moving things around. Ellis was organized and smart with details and planning. Riley was creative and artistic with making things look nice. Layla was a born leader who kept everyone working together. "We're a good team BECAUSE we're different!"

### Scene 18
Layla thought about their Community Market idea and what they learned today. "If we ever do our own market," she said thoughtfully. "We shouldn't all bring the same things to sell there." Their lemonade lesson and today's work both showed the same thing. Different strengths meant different things to give the team.

### Scene 19
The friends imagined what their Community Market could look like someday. "I could make crafts to sell!" Riley said with excitement in her voice. "I could keep track of everything with numbers," Ellis offered helpfully. "I could help carry and set up all the stuff," added Benny with a smile. "And I could organize everything," Layla said.

### Scene 20
Layla leaned in with excitement about their idea growing bigger. "We work so well together now," she said to everyone with a big smile. "What if we actually DID something big? Like a real Community Market for our whole neighborhood?" The idea felt closer than ever before. But first, they needed to learn more about it.
