## LA TAMALADA & THE COMMUNITY MARKET

### Scene 1
It was the big day at last! The friends all went to Frances's warm kitchen early in the morning. The counter was covered with lots of ingredients for tamales. "Before your market," Frances said with a big smile, "I teach you tamalada!"

### Scene 2
"A tamalada is very special to us," Frances said to them all. "My grandmother taught me this long ago in her kitchen. It's not just making food to eat. It's family working together as one team!"

### Scene 3
Frances got a faraway look in her eyes. She remembered her own childhood tamaladas with her family. Many hands working together with love. Laughter filling the warm kitchen. "Today," she said softly, "you are my family!"

### Scene 4
Frances gave each friend a special job to do. Riley would spread masa dough on the husks. Ellis would add the filling inside each one. Benny would wrap them up tight. Layla would check the quality. An assembly line!

### Scene 5
At first, it was really hard. Riley's masa was bumpy. Benny wrapped too loose. Ellis used too much filling. But Frances smiled at them. "Wrong is how we learn!"

### Scene 6
They kept trying. Soon they found their rhythm. Riley's masa got smooth. Ellis used the right amount. Benny's wraps got tight. Layla caught problems. Perfect teamwork!

### Scene 7
Frances looked at their work. "See? None could do this alone," she said. "But together, look what you made!" Dozens of perfect tamales. They felt proud!

### Scene 8
The tamales steamed in a pot. While they waited, Frances said, "This is collaboration. Each person matters. Each job is important. Together you make wonderful things!"

### Scene 9
The friends talked about their market plans together. They had a swap area set up already. An auction corner for special items today. Riley's craft station with beautiful paper flowers. A memory corner to honor loved ones who passed. Frances added more good ideas to help. They were ready to go!

### Scene 10
Frances opened the pot very carefully for them to see. Steam rose up into the warm air. The tamales were cooked just perfect now! "These carry more than just food," she said to them all. "They carry everything you learned from everyone here. Now go share with the whole community!"

### Scene 11
At the park, they set up the market together. Neighbors came to help. Everyone brought something to share. "This is really happening!" Layla said. Mrs. Patterson waved from across the park!

### Scene 12
The market had different areas. A place to swap things. A corner for auctions. Riley's craft station. A memory corner. The tamales stayed warm. Everything was ready!

### Scene 13
Two neighbors couldn't agree on a trade. Layla stepped in. She listened to both. She helped them see both sides. They made a fair trade. Patience worked!

### Scene 14
Ellis ran the auction corner. He explained rules clearly. He kept track of bids. He made sure everything was fair. His thinking worked well. He felt proud!

### Scene 15
Riley led the memory station. Families made paper flowers together. "Can I make one for grandpa?" a child asked. "Of course!" said Riley. "I'll show you!"

### Scene 16
A shy kid stood alone. Benny saw him. He walked over and knelt down. "It's okay to miss people," Benny said. "Making things helps. Sit with me!"

### Scene 17
Frances and friends shared tamales freely. Not sold — shared! Neighbors tasted and smiled. "These are wonderful!" someone said. "How did you make them?" "We made together," Frances said. "As family!"

### Scene 18
Mr. Mason arrived with his cane. He looked at everything they made. The auction. The crafts. The memory corner. His eyes got wet. "You listened. You learned. I'm proud!"

### Scene 19
A young kid came up. "That was so cool!" he said. "Can you teach me?" The friends looked at each other. And smiled big. "Yes," they said. "We can!"

### Scene 20
The sun was setting. People went home happy. Layla said, "We didn't just make a market. We shared everything we learned!" They were teachers now!
