## THE COMMUNITY GARDEN

### Scene 1
The friends walked past empty lot one day. It had tall weeds everywhere growing wild. Trash covered the ground too. "This looks so sad," Layla said with concern. "We could make garden here! A beautiful community place! It would help everyone!"

### Scene 2
They told Frances their idea the next morning. She smiled really big at them warmly. "Community garden is wonderful idea!" she said with joy. "I will help you make it! I teach about plants and growing! I show you everything!" Frances knew lots about gardens already.

### Scene 3
They asked neighbors about using the lot. "Can we make garden here?" they asked politely. The neighbors said yes right away! "What great idea!" they said with smiles. "That will look nice!" Many wanted to help too! Everyone liked the plan.

### Scene 4
Saturday morning came bright and sunny. Everyone came to help work. People brought many tools from home. Shovels and rakes and hoes appeared. Seeds and small plants too. Watering cans for later use. So many helpful neighbors showed up! It made them all happy.

### Scene 5
Frances explained the garden plan to everyone. "Each person gets own little plot. You grow your own food there. You take care of it. Then we share together with everyone!" Everyone liked that plan very much. It sounded fair and fun!

### Scene 6
They started clearing all weeds together. Benny pulled big stubborn weeds out. He was strong and worked hard. Ellis raked dirt very smooth and flat. Riley picked up all trash carefully. She filled big bags full. Layla organized everyone well and kept them working.

### Scene 7
The lot looked so much better now! No more tall weeds covering everything. No more trash making it ugly. The ground was cleared and ready. Time to put seeds in the earth! Everyone felt excited and proud!

### Scene 8
Frances showed them planting. "Make small holes first. Put seeds in holes. Cover with soil gently. Water just a little." Friends tried carefully.

### Scene 9
Each friend picked different plants. Layla picked red tomato seeds. Riley picked colorful flower seeds. Ellis picked orange carrot seeds. Benny picked green pepper seeds. All different picks!

### Scene 10
They planted seeds in neat rows. Made rows straight and nice looking. Used a string to keep them even. Watered every single one gently. "Now we wait patiently," Frances said wisely. "Plants need time to grow strong. Growing takes patience and care."

### Scene 11
Every day they came back. They watered all plants. Pulled out new weeds. Checked for bugs eating plants. They took good care!

### Scene 12
Tiny green shoots came up! The seeds were growing now! "Look at these!" Layla shouted. "They're alive and growing!" Everyone got really excited!

### Scene 13
The plants grew bigger every day. They looked healthier and stronger. Tomatoes started forming on vines. Flowers bloomed in red and yellow colors. Carrots grew down deep underground. Peppers appeared on little bushes. Everything was growing well!

### Scene 14
Finally, vegetables were ready! Time to harvest everything! The friends picked each carefully. Big red tomatoes came off. Orange carrots pulled up. Green peppers picked gently!

### Scene 15
"We grew too much!" Benny said. More than they needed! "What should we do?" Frances smiled wisely. "We share with neighbors!"

### Scene 16
They set up little table at the garden edge. Put vegetables in pretty baskets arranged nicely. Made big sign saying clearly: "Free Garden Food For Everyone!" They painted it bright colors. Neighbors came by to take some vegetables. Everyone said thank you so much!

### Scene 17
"Thank you much!" neighbors said. Some gave them money. "For buying more seeds!" The friends felt proud. They had helped everyone!

### Scene 18
Ellis counted the money. "We have fifteen dollars!" he said. That would buy new seeds. And maybe garden tools. They could grow more!

### Scene 19
"Community garden means sharing," Frances explained. "We share the work. We share the food. We share happy feelings. That's what community means!"

### Scene 20
Layla thought about their market. "Our market should share too. Everyone helps each other. Everyone shares what they do. Everyone wins when we cooperate!" Friends agreed completely. Sharing was best!
