## MR. MASON'S TRUNK

### Scene 1
The friends walked to Mr. Mason's house. He sat on his front porch. "Hello!" he said with a smile. "I need help today. Can you help me?" The friends said yes! They were happy to help.

### Scene 2
They went inside his house. It had so many things! Photos on the walls. Maps everywhere. Small objects on shelves. "You have cool stuff!" Riley said. Mr. Mason smiled big.

### Scene 3
Mr. Mason showed them a ladder. It went up to the attic. "I haven't been up there in years," he said. They climbed up one by one. The attic was full of things!

### Scene 4
The attic had boxes everywhere! Old furniture too. Dust floated in the light. "Wow!" Layla said. "It's like a museum!" The friends looked around. Everything was old and dusty.

### Scene 5
They helped Mr. Mason sort things. "That can stay. That can go," he said. They moved boxes around carefully. They were good helpers! The work was fun to do together.

### Scene 6
Ellis found something big! A big old trunk! It had colorful stickers all over it. Stickers from around the world! "Mr. Mason! What's this?" Ellis called out. Everyone came over.

### Scene 7
Everyone came to look. Mr. Mason smiled big. He touched the trunk. "My old traveling trunk," he said. "This went everywhere with me!" His eyes looked happy. He remembered his trips.

### Scene 8
Mr. Mason opened the trunk. Inside were wrapped objects. Old photos too. "Every object has a story," he said. "Each one teaches a lesson." The friends wanted to know more! They leaned in close.

### Scene 9
He took out a small bag. It had spices inside! The smell filled the attic. "This is from Morocco," he said. "I learned about markets there." The friends smelled the spices. They smelled good!

### Scene 10
"There were market stalls everywhere," Mr. Mason said. "Spices in every color! People calling out loud. Buying and selling." The friends listened. They wanted to hear everything! It sounded so cool.

### Scene 11
"You can look inside," Mr. Mason said. The friends reached in gently. They found many cool things. Things from all around the world! Each one was special. Each one had a story to tell.

### Scene 12
Riley found a wooden fish. "That's from Japan," Mr. Mason said. "From a fish market there. I learned about auctions." Riley held it. It was beautifully carved. She loved the details.

### Scene 13
Benny found a wooden stamp. It had pretty patterns on it. "That's from Italy," Mr. Mason said. "From a bakery. I learned about tradition." Benny examined it. The patterns were nice.

### Scene 14
Ellis found a tiny net. It was woven very well. "That's from a fishing village," Mr. Mason said. "I learned that timing matters." Ellis looked at it. It was so small!

### Scene 15
Layla found a wooden star. It was painted gold and shiny. "That's from Germany," Mr. Mason said. "From a Christmas market there. It was magical!" Layla held it up. It was beautiful!

### Scene 16
Mr. Mason looked at the friends. "These aren't just old things," he said. "They hold important lessons. About how markets work. I've been waiting to share them." The friends got excited.

### Scene 17
The friends got very excited! "Will you tell us the stories?" Layla asked. "Please!" Benny added. They wanted to hear everything! Each object had a lesson. They couldn't wait!

### Scene 18
Mr. Mason smiled at them. "One story at a time," he said. He held up the spice bag. "Let's start with Morocco. That's where our journey begins." The friends got comfortable. They were ready!

### Scene 19
Mr. Mason started describing Morocco. Something magical happened! Colors seemed to appear. Sounds and smells filled their minds. The story came alive! They could see it in their heads.

### Scene 20
The friends sat around the trunk. They were ready to learn! Mr. Mason would teach them about markets. Layla thought: "This will help our own market!" The lessons were beginning! It was going to be fun.
