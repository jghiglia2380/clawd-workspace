## MR. MASON'S TRUNK

### Scene 1
The four friends walked up the path to Mr. Mason's house one warm afternoon. He sat on the front porch with a friendly smile on his face. "Perfect timing!" he called out cheerfully. "I'm cleaning my old attic today. Would you help me reach some high shelves?" The friends agreed to help.

### Scene 2
They followed Mr. Mason inside his cozy house with curiosity growing in their minds. The living room was filled with interesting things from all over the world. Photos covered the walls showing many places he had visited. Maps hung in frames. Little souvenirs sat on every shelf. "You have so many cool things!" Riley said with wonder. "Every one has a story," Mr. Mason replied.

### Scene 3
Mr. Mason led them to a narrow hallway near the back of the house. He pulled down a folding ladder that led up to the attic above them. Dust floated in the beam of light from above. "I haven't been up here in many years," he said quietly with nostalgia. "Let's see what we find together up there."

### Scene 4
One by one, the friends climbed into the dusty attic and looked around everywhere. Boxes and old furniture filled every corner of the space. Sunlight streamed through a small window, making the dust sparkle in the air. "Wow," Layla said in amazement at everything. "It's like a secret museum!" Benny agreed with her.

### Scene 5
They started helping Mr. Mason organize the attic carefully over the next hour. He pointed at boxes, deciding what to keep and what to throw away. The friends moved things without breaking anything important. It felt like regular cleaning at first. "That can go. That stays," Mr. Mason directed them. "Be careful with that one."

### Scene 6
Ellis moved an old blanket in the back corner and discovered something underneath. It was a large leather trunk covered with colorful stickers from around the world. He could see Paris, Cairo, Tokyo, and Morocco on the labels. "Mr. Mason?" Ellis called with excitement. "What's THIS thing over here in the corner?"

### Scene 7
Everyone gathered around the trunk to see what Ellis had found there. Mr. Mason's face changed when he saw it after so many years. Joy and memory mixed together in his expression. He knelt down and touched the old leather gently with his hand. "My traveling trunk," he said softly with emotion in his voice.

### Scene 8
Mr. Mason opened the trunk's heavy lid carefully as the friends watched. Inside were objects wrapped in cloth, old photographs, and small treasures from around the world. "Every object in here is a memory," Mr. Mason explained with a smile. "A lesson learned. A story waiting to be told." The friends leaned closer.

### Scene 9
Mr. Mason lifted out a small leather pouch and held it up for everyone. He opened it, and the smell of exotic spices filled the attic air. He breathed in deeply and smiled. "This is from a market in Morocco," he said. "The spice market... where I learned to negotiate fair prices with merchants."

### Scene 10
Mr. Mason's eyes got distant as he remembered the experience clearly. "Stalls as far as you could see," he began describing the scene. "Spices in every color everywhere you looked. Merchants calling out to everyone passing by." His voice carried them to that faraway place. The friends listened closely, wanting to hear everything.

### Scene 11
"Go ahead, look inside the trunk," Mr. Mason invited them with a warm smile. "But carefully — these are more than just things. They're teachers of important lessons." The friends reached into the trunk with gentle hands. Each one discovered something different among the treasures. Every item came from a different country.

### Scene 12
Riley lifted out a small carved wooden fish and examined it in her hands. Mr. Mason nodded when he saw it. "That's from Japan," he explained with a smile. "The fish market in Tokyo. I watched an auction that changed everything I knew about selling." Riley held the fish carefully, admiring the craftsmanship and detail.

### Scene 13
Benny found a wooden stamp with carved patterns and held it up. "What's this used for?" he asked with interest. "That's a bread stamp from Italy," Mr. Mason said with a smile. "From a family bakery that's been running for three generations. They taught me lessons about costs." Benny examined it closely.

### Scene 14
Ellis discovered a tiny fishing net in the trunk and picked it up. "This came from a Mediterranean fishing village," Mr. Mason explained with fond memories. "I helped a fisherman sell his catch one morning. I learned that timing matters — some things can't wait to be sold later." Ellis held the delicate net gently.

### Scene 15
Layla held up a beautiful hand-carved wooden star that caught the light. Mr. Mason's face lit up at the sight of it. "That's from a Christmas market in Germany," he said warmly. "Snow falling, lights glowing, handmade treasures everywhere. Pure magic and beauty." Layla admired how it sparkled.

### Scene 16
Mr. Mason sat back and looked at the friends gathered around the trunk. His face became serious but kind as he spoke. "These aren't just old things from my travels," he said carefully. "They're lessons I've been saving for the right people. I think I've been waiting for you all along."

### Scene 17
The friends were captivated by the trunk and its treasures from around the world. They leaned forward to hear more stories from Mr. Mason's travels. "Will you tell us all the stories?" Layla asked hopefully with excitement. "Please tell us everything!" Benny added to her request. The treasures felt like keys.

### Scene 18
Mr. Mason smiled at their excitement and eagerness. "One story at a time," he agreed with a gentle nod. "Each object has a lesson to teach about markets and economics. Let's start with the spices first." He held up the pouch again. "The markets of Morocco... that's where our journey begins today."

### Scene 19
As Mr. Mason described the Moroccan market in detail, something magical seemed to happen. The edges of the attic appeared to soften slightly. Colors and sounds from a distant place seemed to shimmer around them. The memory was beginning to come alive in front of their eyes.

### Scene 20
The friends settled around the trunk, ready to travel through Mr. Mason's memories from long ago. They were about to learn from markets all over the world. And in Layla's mind, their Community Market idea grew stronger. Learning from the best markets would help them build their own successfully.
